FOLD MAP

How a flat sheet becomes a notebook

  • STEP 01Choose your paper.Kozo washi, 90gsm. Nothing thinner holds a crease.
  • STEP 02Valley fold, edge to edge.One sharp crease down the center. Burnish it with bone, not thumb.
  • STEP 03Mountain fold the spine.Fold back against the grain. This is the fold that lasts decades.
  • STEP 04Crease, rest, repeat.Seven folds, thirty seconds of rest between. Paper remembers patience.

THE RULE

Every product ships flat with its crease map printed inside. You fold it. That's the point — the object is the instructions.
