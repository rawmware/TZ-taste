A room built for one purpose

The Athenaeum was founded on a simple wager: that a city which reads together stays together. The stacks hold fiction A–C on the third stack to the left, poetry under the east window, and the entire reference collection within arm's reach of a green-shaded lamp.

No one will shush you here — the hush is built into the oak. Bring a pencil, not a pen. The marginalia in these books goes back a century, and some of it is better than the text.
