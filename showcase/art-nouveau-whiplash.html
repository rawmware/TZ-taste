The Linden Playhouse

EST. 1911

Four productions, one hundred and fourteen years of candlelight and curtain calls. This season we stage the orchard, the winter, the clockmaker, and the road home.

READ THE SEASON

The Season

I

OCT 17 – NOV 09
THU–SUN · 19:30

The Glass Orchard

A family returns to sell the orchard and discovers the trees have opinions. Our autumn opener, staged in the round, with real apples and one very patient dog.

II

DEC 05 – DEC 28
WED–SUN · 19:30

A Winter in Arles

Two painters, one yellow house, and a winter that refuses to end. The Linden's holiday tradition — bring someone you argue beautifully with.

III

FEB 13 – MAR 08
THU–SUN · 19:30

The Clockmaker's Daughter

She inherits the shop, the gears, and every stopped clock in the county. A fable about time, told with eleven working timepieces on stage.

IV

APR 10 – MAY 03
THU–SUN · 19:30

Song of the Salt Road

A traveling company crosses the desert with a wagon of instruments and a map drawn from memory. Our season closer, with live music throughout.

The House Rules

Seats are limited. Reserve yours. Coats are checked, phones are silenced, and the bar opens one hour before curtain.
