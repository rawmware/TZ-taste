THE POT

Master Sato has run the same tonkotsu pot since 1986. It has never been fully emptied — every night's broth starts with yesterday's. Regulars swear they can taste 1989 in it. They're right.

The noodles are pulled at 10pm, the eggs marinated for exactly 26 hours, and the chili oil is a family secret that died with grandma and was resurrected from a stained notebook.

COUNTER RULES

  • Slurp loudly. Silence insults the noodles.
  • Buy your ticket first, sit second, talk never (to the chef).
  • Eighteen seats means eighteen fates. Don't linger past your last sip.
  • Photos of the ramen: yes. Photos of Master Sato: he will end you.
  • Cash only. The machine eats bills, not excuses.
