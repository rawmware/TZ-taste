Brown-butter shortbread

The recipe customers ask for. Now it's yours.

  • Unsalted butter, browned and cooled225 g
  • Golden caster sugar100 g
  • Plain flour, sifted300 g
  • Fine sea salt1 tsp
  • Vanilla bean paste1 tsp

Rub together, press into a lined tin, dock with a fork, and bake at 160°C for 35 minutes. Slice while warm. Share if you must.
