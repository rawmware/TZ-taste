From the keeper's log

OCT 12, 1962 — KEEPER E. VOSS

Fog for the ninth straight day. The signal sounds every 40 seconds and I have started answering it. Polished the lens twice. The lens does not need it. I needed it.

NOV 03, 1978 — KEEPER M. OKAFOR

Storm took the flagpole again. Third one this decade. The gulls watched the whole thing and did not help. Repainted the door red, since the sea keeps trying to take the color.

JAN 28, 1994 — KEEPER R. LINDQVIST

A ship radioed to thank us. First thank-you in eleven years. Wrote it in the log twice so nobody thinks I imagined it. Burned an extra lamp in celebration.
