Liner notes

Personnel: Elias Marlowe, piano; Ruth Okafor, bass; Sam Reyes, drums. Recorded live at The Half Step, Portland, Maine, September 2026. Engineered by June Park. The piano is a 1962 Baldwin, tuned every Thursday whether it needs it or not.

"The second set is where the night actually happens. Everything before that is the band deciding whether they trust you." — Elias Marlowe

Doors at seven, kitchen until ten. Students with ID drink coffee free on Sundays. The listening room is downstairs; the bar is upstairs; the line between them dissolves around the second chorus.
