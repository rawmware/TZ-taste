◈ BEFORE YOU SCREAM

Is it too scary for kids?
Under 12 must bring an adult they trust. Under 8 must bring an adult the manor trusts.

What if it rains?
The haunts run in all weather. Ghosts don't check the forecast, and neither do we.

Can I get a refund?
Only if you can prove you weren't scared. Nobody has managed it since 1987.
