Brew.
Sit.
The tea is enough.

Twelve seats. One kettle. No music, no menus shouted across the room — the pour is the conversation.

THE POURING LIST

Sencha

80°C · 2 MIN

Steamed green from Uji, whisked bright. Tastes like the field it grew in, which is the whole point.

Hojicha

90°C · 1 MIN

Roasted until it smells like toast and autumn. The cup we hand to people who say they don't like tea.

Gyokuro

55°C · 3 MIN

Shade-grown, served in small cups, sipped slower than you want to. Worth the wait; the wait is the lesson.

Genmaicha

85°C · 2 MIN

Green tea with toasted rice. Warm, plain, honest — the house pour, bottomless on rainy days.
