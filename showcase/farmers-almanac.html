The Old Farmer’s Desk MOON · WAXING GIBBOUS

FALL NUMBER · SET BY HAND · 134TH PRINTING

Plant peas when the forsythia blooms. Not before.

The moon is waxing, so this week belongs to the above-ground crops — the ones that reach for the light. Roots wait for the wane.

Frost is the real boss of the garden, and the almanac is the only calendar that admits it. Last frost here: May 12, give or take a week. First frost: October 8, sooner if the north wind has its way.

Weather wisdom for the week: when the fog lifts before eight, the afternoon turns fair. When the crows gather on the wire, rain by Thursday.
