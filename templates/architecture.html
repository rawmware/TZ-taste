Manifesto

We design buildings that explain themselves — no decoration, no apology.

Every line must earn its place. If a detail doesn't serve structure, light, or the people inside, it doesn't survive the review. Our drawings are famous for what isn't in them.

Buildings are slow media. A poster lasts a season; a housing block lasts generations. We design for the tenth year, not the opening photo — materials that age honestly, plans that adapt.

What we build

S—01

Culture

Libraries, museums, theaters — buildings the public owns emotionally. Designed for the school trip and the quiet Tuesday alike.

 S—02

Housing

From 96-unit blocks to infill courtyards. Light in every room, storage where it matters, no "luxury" that isn't.

 S—03

Workplace

Offices people choose to come to: daylight, timber, and plans that survive reorganizations.

 S—04

Education

Schools as civic anchors — robust, joyful, and cheap to run for fifty years.

 S—05

Urban

Market halls, stations, public realm. The city between the buildings is also architecture.

 S—06

Research

Our open detail library and material studies — free, because the industry is better when details are shared.

The studio

Founded 2009 by Mara Voss and Daan Verhoeven in a former print works in Rotterdam-Noord. We are now 24 architects, 3 model-makers, and 1 very opinionated office dog.

We publish our detail library open-source, teach a studio at TU Delft every spring, and review every project at the same long table — partners and interns, one vote each on the geometry.
