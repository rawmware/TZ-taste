A room that smells like Sunday at nonna's.

Elena Marchetti opened Osteria Alba with her brother Marco after ten years cooking in Bologna. The recipes are her grandmother's; the room is pure Portland — brick, low light, and a wood oven Marco built himself over one very long winter.

Everything is made in-house: pasta rolled each afternoon, bread from the oven's first heat, gelato churned to order. The menu is short because the market is the menu — if the fishermen didn't bring it, we're not serving it.

— Elena & Marco

Sixty wines. Zero intimidation.

Our list leans Italian and natural — small producers, honest farming, labels with stories. Every wine is available by the glass because choosing shouldn't require a commitment.

  • Ask for Lucia — she'll find your bottle in three questions or less.
  • Bring your own on Tuesdays: no corkage, ever.
  • The orange wine flight: three pours, one very good evening.

The back room, yours for the evening.

Behind the kitchen sits our fourteen-seat cantina — stone walls, one long table, and a menu Elena writes for your table alone.
