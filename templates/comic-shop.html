House rules

  • Ask before you open a sealed book — we have reader copies of almost everything.
  • The manga corner has beanbags. They are first-come, first-sat.
  • Kids' corner is genuinely all-ages. We curate it like parents.
  • We buy collections. Bring the longboxes; we'll make tea and an offer.
  • No spoilers out loud on Wednesdays. This is enforced.
