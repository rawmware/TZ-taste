Made slowly,
on purpose.

Every Vesper piece passes through eleven hands in our Porto atelier before it earns the label. We cut in small runs — never more than three hundred of anything — and we keep every pattern, so your coat can be repaired in ten years, not replaced.

Our fabrics come from five mills we have worked with since the house was founded. We know the weavers by name. That is not marketing; it is simply how cloth this good gets made.
