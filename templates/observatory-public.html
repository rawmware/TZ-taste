Three domes, one lawn

Dress for 20 degrees colder than town.

The hill is always colder than you think, and you'll be standing still looking up. Blankets are provided; dignity is your own.

Tickets $18 · under 12s $9 · members free. Gates open at 7:30pm, first views at dark. Red flashlights only — white light ruins everyone's night vision, and we'll lend you a red one.

Getting here

Meridian Hill Road, 40 minutes from downtown. Free parking in the lower lot; the walk up is part of it.

Good to know

No white flashlights. Red ones lent free at the gate. Warm drinks in the visitor center all night.

Courses

Telescope Basics, first Tuesdays monthly, $40. Leave able to find Saturn alone.
