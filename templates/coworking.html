The silence policy.

Posted by the door, enforced by culture. It's why members stay for years.

  1. No calls in the Long Room. Phone booths on every floor; the deep-work floor stays library-quiet.
  2. Headphones mean do not disturb. It's the house signal. Respect it absolutely.
  3. Keyboards stay quiet after midnight. We stock silent switches at the front desk — free to borrow.
  4. The kitchen is for conversation. Loud, happy, unlimited. Sound stays behind its door.

Asked at the door

Questions

Is it really open 24/7?

Really. Your keycard works at 4am on a Tuesday in February. Night staff are members on a rota — there's always a human in the building, and the coffee machine never sleeps.

What if I need to take calls during the day?

That's what the Night Owl plan and the six phone booths are for. Daytime callers usually pick the Loft or a booth — the Long Room stays silent around the clock.

Can I receive mail and packages?

Residents and Studios get a real street address and parcel receiving. Drifters and Night Owls can use the address for occasional packages — just warn us about the large ones.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. Every plan is month-to-month with 30 days' notice. The average member stays 2.4 years, which we take as a compliment and a responsibility.

Getting here at 2 a.m.

The night shift only works if you can actually reach us. Grünerløkka is one of the few Oslo neighborhoods where "open all night" applies to the streets too.

  • Night bus21N runs Fri–Sat all night, stops 3 minutes' walk away. Weeknights, the last regular bus is 00:40.
  • BikeCovered bike parking in the courtyard, plus a repair stand. The ride along the Akerselva is lit all night.
  • CarStreet parking is free 8pm–8am. We validate the Grünerløkka garage for day-pass holders.
  • SafetyWell-lit entrance, staffed 24/7, and a walking-buddy board for the late shift. Ask anyone.
