Custom work

Your idea, drawn twice, inked once

Custom pieces start with a drawing fee that comes off the tattoo. We draw it, you approve it, we ink it.

  1. Bring the idea

    Photos, scribbles, a story — anything. Fifteen minutes, free, at the counter or by email.

  2. We draw it

    Your artist draws two directions within a week. The $50 drawing fee is deducted from the tattoo price.

  3. You approve it

    See it on paper, see it on skin with a stencil. Change anything before the needle comes out.

  4. We ink it

    Booked in sessions that respect your skin and your schedule. Touch-ups free within a year.

Aftercare, the short version

  1. Leave the wrap on for the drive home, not the weekend.
  2. Wash gently, pat dry, thin layer of ointment.
  3. No pools, no sun, no picking for two weeks.
  4. Itchy means healing. Call us if it's hot or spreading.
