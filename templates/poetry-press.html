Submissions

the window opens in october.

We read chapbook manuscripts (24–48 pages) each October. No reading fee, ever. Response by February — a real letter, not a form.

What we're hungry for: work with a pulse. Formal or free, quiet or loud — surprise us, but mean it.

What we're full on: pandemic diaries, AI-generated anything, and poems about writing poems (we've published our lifetime quota).
