How an evening works

One fee. The whole night.

The table fee

$8 / person · entire evening
  • Unlimited games from the 800-title library
  • A game teacher at your table, on request
  • Free re-teach if you forget the rules mid-game
  • Under 12s play free with a paying adult
Reserve a table

  1. Walk in or book ahead

    Friday nights fill by 7pm — book online. Weeknights, just walk in.

  2. Tell us your group

    How many, how competitive, how long you've got. We'll pull three perfect options.

  3. Learn in ten minutes

    A staffer teaches at your table while the snacks arrive. No rulebook homework.

  4. Play till close

    Swap games freely all night. The fee doesn't change — stay for one more.
