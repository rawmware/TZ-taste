Salt & Foam is a ten-barrel beachside brewery two blocks from T-Street. We brew what we want to drink after a surf: bright, bitter, cold, and gone by sunset. Sixteen taps, a wood-fired oven, and a patio that faces due west.

Started in a garage. Still smells like it.

2014: two surf buddies, one homebrew kit, and a garage that the neighbors complained about twice.

2016: we signed the lease on the old bait shop on El Camino Real and built the brewhouse ourselves — mostly correctly.

Today: ten barrels, sixteen taps, and a wood-fired oven run by our friend Rosa, whose margherita has its own fan club. We still surf before the morning mash. The beer is better for it.

Good questions

Before you paddle out

Do you fill growlers from other breweries?

Happily. Clean it first — we'll rinse it again anyway. $14 for most fills, $18 for the stout and the rotating sour.

Is the patio really dog-friendly?

The patio, yes; inside, no (health code, not our rule). Water bowls are always out, and the staff keeps treats behind the bar.

Do you distribute?

Foghorn and Pier Pils go to about forty bars between Dana Point and Oceanside. Everything else is taproom-only — another reason to visit.

Can I buy a keg for a party?

Yes — sixth-barrels of anything on the board, $120–$160 with a $50 refundable deposit. Order a week ahead.
