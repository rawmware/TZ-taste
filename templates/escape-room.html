INTERROGATION TRANSCRIPT — EXCERPTS

Questions we get asked.

Q · 01

Are we actually locked in?

No. Every door opens from the inside with a push — the "locked" part is theatrical. Anyone can step out anytime; your team keeps playing.

Q · 02

What if we get stuck?

Your game master watches the whole time and nudges before frustration sets in. Hints are unlimited and judgment-free.

Q · 03

Is it scary?

The Alchemist's Basement is tense; the other two aren't scary at all. Nothing jumps out. We promise, in writing, on the waiver.

Q · 04

Can kids play?

Ages 10 and up with an adult. The Curator's Office is the family favorite — no reading level required beyond "can follow a treasure map."
