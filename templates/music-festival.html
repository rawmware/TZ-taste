QUICK ANSWERS

Can I leave and come back?−

Yes — wristbands allow re-entry until 11pm daily. After that, you're in for the night (the Neon Tent runs till 2am, so you won't mind).

What about rain?+

Two stages are fully covered, the show goes on rain or shine, and ponchos are $3 at every merch stand. Mud is part of the aesthetic.

Is there an age limit?+

No — it's all ages. Under 12s get in free with a ticket-holding adult, and there's a family field with earlier camping quiet hours.

Can I get a refund?+

Full refunds until June 1, and ticket transfers are free anytime through your account. After June 1, we'll help you resell at face value.
