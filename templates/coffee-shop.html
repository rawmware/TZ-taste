From cherry to cup

Thursday is roast day

One day a week, the whole shop smells like possibility. Here's how a batch goes.

6:00 AM

The roaster warms up

Our 12kg Probat takes an hour to reach temperature. Ingrid arrives with rye bread; the first cup of yesterday's roast gets us going.

7:30 AM

Batch by batch

Green coffee goes in at 200°C. Twelve minutes of watching, listening for first crack, and logging every degree. No two batches get the same profile twice without earning it.

11:00 AM

Cupping

Every batch gets cupped before it gets bagged. If it doesn't taste like the farm we bought it from, it becomes staff coffee — never retail.

12:30 PM

On the shelf

Bagged, labeled with the roast date, and on the shelf by lunch. Coffee this fresh doesn't need a "best by" — it has a "roasted on."

Retail shelf

Take the roast home

250g bags, roasted Thursday, shipped Friday. Whole bean — we'll grind it in the shop if you ask nicely.
