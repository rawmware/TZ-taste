Borrowing

Like books, but you plant them.

Free with any library card. No fines, no due dates — just one gentle expectation.

  1. Check out seeds

    Take up to 8 packets per visit from the catalog drawers. Staff will help you pick varieties for your soil and sunlight.

  2. Grow them out

    Plant, water, weed, harvest. Growing guides are tucked in every packet — written by local gardeners, not seed companies.

  3. Save some seed

    Let your best plant go to seed at season's end. The packet explains how — most varieties take ten minutes.

  4. Bring seeds back

    Return a labeled envelope of saved seed. It goes back in the drawer for next year's gardeners. The collection grows because you do.
