Chapter I · The Shop

A bookstore for people who read past midnight.

Obsidian & Oak has stood on Essex Street since 1987 — three floors of new, used, and genuinely rare books, a resident shop cat named Marginalia, and booksellers who will argue with you, affectionately, about endings. We don't do algorithms. We do opinions, strongly held and freely given.

Chapter V½ · The Young

The children's room is downstairs.

The whole third floor belongs to readers under twelve: picture books at knee height, a reading nook shaped like a ship, and a wall where kids review books in crayon — the most honest criticism in the building.

Saturday story hour at 11am. Birthday parties among the stacks (ten kids max, cake allowed, glitter negotiable).

Chapter V¾ · Sell Us Books

Your shelves are our inventory.

We buy used books every afternoon, 1–5pm. Bring a box; leave with store credit (50% of what we'll price them at) or cash (30%). No appointment needed for under two boxes.
