Why the desert

Three hundred days of sun, zero days of compromise.

Palm Springs isn't a market — it's a pilgrimage. Buyers come from Los Angeles, San Francisco, and New York for the same reason the architects did in the 1950s: light you can't get anywhere else, and houses designed to drink it in.

Inventory is finite — they aren't making more 1960s post-and-beams. Prices have compounded at 7% annually for a decade. We know, because we've sold through all of it.

New to the valley? Start with the Modernism Week tours each February — eleven days of open houses that double as a crash course — then call us before the spring rush. The best houses never reach the portals.
