How a sitting goes

Slow on purpose

  1. Tea first

    You arrive, we talk. I learn what the photographs are for and who they're really of. The camera stays in its bag.

  2. The awkward forty-five

    Everyone is stiff at first. I photograph through it — most people relax somewhere around frame thirty.

  3. The real twenty

    Then it happens: you forget the camera. That's when I stop directing and start watching.

  4. The contact sheet

    A week later, we sit with the prints and you circle your frames. You always know which ones. Everyone does.
