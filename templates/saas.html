FAQ

Asked by every ops lead

Do we need an engineer to set this up?+

No. If you can log into Stripe and Salesforce, you can connect Northloop. OAuth does the heavy lifting; CSV upload covers everything else. Median time to first live number is under an hour.

How is this different from our BI tool?+

BI tools answer questions you think to ask. Northloop watches the numbers and tells you when something moved, with the reason attached. Most customers keep both — Northloop is the page they actually open.

Where does our data live?+

In your region, encrypted at rest and in transit, SOC 2 Type II certified. We cache aggregates for speed; raw records stay in your warehouse if you prefer that setup.

What happens when the trial ends?+

Nothing dramatic. You pick a plan or the account goes read-only — your data stays accessible for 90 days either way, and exports are always free.
