What we do

Four ways we show up

Everything is free, no ID or paperwork required. If you're hungry, you're welcome — that's the whole policy.

Saturday Market

Fresh produce, bread, dairy, and pantry staples — take what you need, leave what you can. Kids pick a free piece of fruit at the door.

Saturdays
9am – 12pm

Community Garden

Two acres behind Riverside Hall, tended by 80 families. Beginners get a plot, seeds, and a patient neighbor to show them the ropes.

Open daily
dawn – dusk

Cooking Classes

Free Tuesday-night classes: stretching a chicken three ways, cooking for one, kids' lunchbox basics. Aprons provided, judgment absent.

Tuesdays
6pm – 7:30pm

Senior Deliveries

Volunteers deliver groceries to 140 homebound seniors every Thursday — and stay for a chat, which matters as much as the food.

Thursdays
10am – 2pm

Two ways to help

Give your Saturday

No experience needed — we'll pair you with a veteran volunteer for your first shift. Most people start with the market and end up staying for lunch.

Give what you can

$25 stocks the market shelves for a family of four for a week. Monthly givers keep the van fueled and the garden watered.
