QUICK ANSWERS

Can I get in after 2am?+

Yes — general and final-release tickets are valid all night. Last entry is 04:00. Early Current tickets must arrive before midnight.

What's the photo policy?+

Strict. No photos or video on the dancefloors — our stewards will ask you to stop, then ask you to leave. The chill roof is photo-friendly.

Is there re-entry?+

Yes, with a wristband stamp until 03:00. The yard has heated tents, food, and water, so most people don't bother.

What if it sells out?+

Join the waitlist — we release returned tickets at face value every Friday at noon. Never buy above face value; fakes get turned away at the door.

Can I bring my own earplugs?+

Please do — but you won't need to. Free high-fidelity earplugs at every door, plus a quiet welfare room if the night gets loud in the wrong way.

Is there anywhere to eat?+

The yard hosts six food stalls from 22:00 to 04:00 — noodles, flatbreads, and one legendary grilled-cheese truck. Free tap water everywhere, always.
