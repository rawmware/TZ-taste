Meet Miso.
She runs the place.

Our giant Pacific octopus arrived as a rescue in 2023 — tangled in fishing line, missing an arm. She has since regrown the arm, learned to open every latch we own, and developed strong opinions about which aquarist feeds her.

ADMIT ONE — SECTION 04 · THE WORK
"Every animal here is a rescue, a rehab case, or part of a breeding program. If we can't give it a better life than the ocean gave it, we don't keep it."
— DR. LENA OKAFOR, DIRECTOR OF ANIMAL CARE

We're a working waterfront aquarium, not a theme park. Our seal cove rehabilitates stranded pups for release — 31 released since 2019. Our kelp research feeds into Gulf of Maine restoration projects.

Ticket revenue pays for fish food, vet bills, and the enormous electric bill. Membership pays for the research. The gift shop pays for Miso's puzzle boxes, which she destroys weekly.
