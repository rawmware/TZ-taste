The club costs nothing because the club owns nothing. We meet in public parks, drink from public fountains, and the website runs on a volunteer's server. Here's what membership actually means:

  • 01Show up. That's the membership form.
  • 02Bring water in summer and a light in winter. We have spares of both.
  • 03Nobody gets dropped. The last runner gets the loudest cheer.
  • 04Optional: the group chat, the race plans, the post-run breakfast crew.
