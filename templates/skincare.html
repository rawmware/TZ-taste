How to use it

The protocol. Three steps.

AM and PM. Sixty seconds, twice a day. That's the whole routine.

Step 01

Cleanse

One pump of F04-C, lukewarm water, 30 seconds. Pat — don't rub — dry.

F04-C · Gentle Gel Cleanser
Step 02

Treat

Three drops of F04-S on dry skin. Wait 60 seconds. Expect a mild tingle in week one.

F04-S · 15% Vitamin C Serum
Step 03

Seal

A pea of F04-M locks it in. AM: follow with SPF 30+ — the serum is not sunscreen.

F04-M · Barrier Moisturizer
