STUDIO NOTES

Four things regulars know.

NOTE 01

Your first bowl will be ugly

Everyone's is. Keep it anyway — in six months you'll love it precisely because it's lopsided.

NOTE 02

Wipe the wheel. Every time.

Clay dries into concrete. The next person's masterpiece starts with your sponge work. Thank you.

NOTE 03

Glazing has office hours

The glaze room closes at 8 PM sharp so the glazes can rest. They're temperamental. So are we, after 8.

NOTE 04

Biscuit is not for sale

The studio pug has been offered treats, belly rubs, and one serious adoption bid. He stays. So will you.
