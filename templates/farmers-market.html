The market doesn't hibernate.

When the park goes quiet, we move indoors. The winter market runs first Saturdays, November through April, at the Memorial Auditorium — storage crops, frozen berries, cheese, meat, maple everything, and the seed swap in February.

Good to know

Questions

Do vendors take cards?

Most do, but the market runs smoothest on cash — there's an ATM at the park's north corner. EBT/SNAP is doubled on produce up to $20 at the info tent.

What happens in bad weather?

We open rain or shine — only lightning or a state emergency closes us. Check the info line by 7am Saturday if the sky looks serious.

Can I bring my dog?

Leashed dogs are welcome around the park's edge, but please keep them out of the stall rows — too many delicious ankles at nose height.

Is the produce really all local?

Every vendor grows or makes what they sell within 60 miles, verified by annual farm visits. If you ever doubt a stall, ask at the info tent — we'll show you the paperwork.

Saturday, 8am. Bring a tote.

The early hour gets the heirlooms; the last hour gets the deals. Either way, you leave with something that was in the ground yesterday.
