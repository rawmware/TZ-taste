Started with a family recipe and a borrowed truck.

Rosa started Masa Mobile in 2019 with her grandmother's birria recipe, a truck with 200,000 miles on it, and a folding table. The truck now has 260,000 miles. The recipe hasn't changed.

Everything is prepped each morning at the commissary on 5th Street — the beef braises for six hours before the truck even rolls.
