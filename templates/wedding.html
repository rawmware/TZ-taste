Before you ask

Questions, answered

Do you travel for weddings?

Constantly. Paris, Provence, Tuscany, the Hudson Valley — about half our weddings are destination. Travel is billed at cost, planned transparently, and we've never missed a flight that mattered.

Can we keep our own vendors?

Of course. We'll be honest about which ones we'd keep and which ones we'd gently replace, and why. No commission, no kickbacks — our vendor list is earned, not bought.

What if it rains?

Every outdoor wedding gets a real Plan B, designed with the same care as Plan A — not a tent apology. In twelve years we've executed eleven rain plans. Ten couples say they preferred the backup.

How far ahead should we book?

Full planning: 12–18 months. Design: 6–9 months. Month-of: the moment you realize you want to enjoy your own wedding — usually about 90 days out, sometimes 30.
