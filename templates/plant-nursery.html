THE SHORT VERSION

Why native, in three sentences each.

1

They feed the neighborhood

A native oak hosts over 500 species of caterpillars; a non-native ginkgo hosts five. Birds raise their young on caterpillars. The math is not subtle.

2

They survive your neglect

These plants evolved for this rainfall, this soil, this winter. Once established — usually one season — most need no watering, no fertilizer, no fussing.

3

They're honest about straight species

We sell the wild type, not the double-flowered cultivar the pollinators can't use. If a plant can't feed anything, it doesn't earn bench space.
