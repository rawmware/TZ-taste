Issue Nº 341 · Sept 27, 2026

The Arithmetic of Enough

On the number that ends the chase — and why nobody believes theirs.

9 min read · 2,140 words

Every wealthy person I know has a number. Almost none of them believe they've reached it. The number moves — that's its job. It is not a finish line; it is a treadmill with good marketing.

This week I asked thirty readers to write down their number, seal it, and revisit it in a year. Twenty-six moved it upward. Including, I'm embarrassed to say, mine.

So here's the exercise I now do every January: I write the number, then I write what changes the day after I hit it. The second list is always shorter than I expect, and always more honest than the first…
