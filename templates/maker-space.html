FIG. 4 — NON-NEGOTIABLES

Safety is the membership

The yellow boxes are the rules that get your keycard suspended. Everything else is a suggestion. We are friendly about all of it exactly once.

RULE 01

Training before touching

Any machine marked T in the inventory needs its evening class first. Classes run Tuesdays and Thursdays, 6 PM, and take about two hours. No test, no quiz — we just need to see you run it safely once.

RULE 02

Eyes, ears, lungs

Safety glasses in the wood and metal shops, always — we have a wall of them, take a pair. Hearing protection at the table saw and router. Respirator required for MDF on the laser and any resin work.

RULE 03

Clean 15 minutes

Every session ends with 15 minutes of cleanup — sweep your station, empty the dust collector if it's full, put tools back. The next person's project starts where yours ended.

RULE 04

Never alone on the big three

Table saw, CNC, and welder: never run solo. If the shop is empty, those machines stay off. This rule has no exceptions and no appeal process.

FIG. 5 — GETTING HERE
