Sheet 04 — The fine print, enlarged

RULES OF
THE KITCHEN.

Four rules. They are the reason 140 businesses have launched here without a single failed inspection.

RULE 01

Label everything

Your name, the date, what's inside. Unlabeled food gets tossed on Fridays — no appeals, no exceptions, no matter how good the jam was.

RULE 02

Clean as you go

The next shift starts the minute yours ends. Stations wiped, floors swept, dishes through the machine. The checklist is on the wall; it takes 20 minutes.

RULE 03

Book it or lose it

Stations are reserved in the app. No-shows get charged; two no-shows lose the shift. Somebody else's hot sauce empire is waiting.

RULE 04

Ask about allergens

This is a shared facility — nuts, dairy, gluten, and sesame all move through here. Your labels must say so, by law and by decency.
