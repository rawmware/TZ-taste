The show

Reported like a newsroom.
Told like a late-night call.

Every season, host Mara Ellison and producer Dev Okafor spend six months inside one cold case — courthouse basements, kitchen-table interviews, FOIA requests that take longer than the investigation did.

We don't solve cases for sport. Cold Trail exists because families write to us after every other door has closed. We publish what we can verify, we say plainly what we can't, and we hand everything we find back to the people it belongs to.

Season 6 follows the 1998 disappearance of Ruth Calloway, a night-shift nurse last seen leaving Harrow Creek General at 2:40 a.m. The file was shelved in 2003. We started with the duty roster and didn't stop.

New episodes drop every Tuesday. Case files, maps, and source documents publish alongside each episode for listeners who want to read along.

Fourteen years covering courts and crime for the Portland Press Herald before trading the newsroom for a microphone. Mara does the interviews nobody else gets — because she shows up twice, and brings the file both times.
