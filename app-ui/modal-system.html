One overlay, four manners

Seedling's modal system: the same dimmed backdrop every time, a different shape of asking. Open each one — click the backdrop, or press Esc, and notice where your focus lands.

01

Confirm

A destructive choice, stated plainly. Two buttons, the safe one on the right, nothing else to read.

02

Form

Three fields, one job: log a watering. Focus lands in the first field; Enter saves.

03

Drawer

A detail sheet that slides in from the right and keeps the page behind it visible and dimmed.

04

Alert

Something needs attention — the backup paused. One acknowledgment, no decisions to make.

Keyboard contract for all four: Esc closes · Tab stays inside the dialog · focus returns to the button that opened it.
