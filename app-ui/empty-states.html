Reading these states

  • One action, not two. An empty state is a fork, not a menu. Pick the single most likely next step and say it as a verb.
  • Name what happened. "Nothing filed under that" beats "No results found" — the first one sounds like a person checked.
  • Illustrate, don't decorate. Each drawing is one idea: a lens that found nothing, a bell at rest, a page waiting for ink.
