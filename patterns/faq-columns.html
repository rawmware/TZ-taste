Q.

Is this going to replace someone on my team?

No — it replaces the triage shift nobody wanted. Your team stops sorting mail and starts answering the messages that matter. Headcount stays; busywork goes.

Q.

How long until it sounds like me?

About two weeks of corrections. The first drafts are decent; by day fourteen most users approve without edits. The impatient can fast-track it with thirty minutes of focused training.

Q.

What if it writes something wrong?

Every automatic send has a ten-minute undo window, and sensitive senders stay on manual approval forever. In three years, no customer has reported an unapproved send.

Q.

Can I leave whenever I want?

Two clicks, no retention call, exports free for thirty days after. We'd rather earn the renewal than trap the customer — it's worked so far.
