faq(1) · user manual

$ helpdesk — bash

$ faq --topic billing

Q: Can I switch plans mid-month?

A: Yes. Upgrades apply instantly; downgrades land on the next cycle. Everything is pro-rated to the day — no rounding in our favor.

$ faq --topic setup

Q: How long does setup take?

A: Under 30 minutes for most teams. Connect over OAuth, approve twelve drafts, then run one supervised week before anything auto-sends.

$ faq --topic privacy

Q: Do you train models on my messages?

A: No. Your messages train your voice profile only. No selling, no sharing, SOC 2 audited — the audit specifically checks this.

$ faq --topic cancel

Q: What happens when I cancel?

A: Exports stay free for 30 days, then we purge everything and email you proof. Two clicks, no retention call, no guilt trip.
