Help center

Find your answer.

  • Can I switch plans mid-month?

    Yes — upgrades apply instantly, downgrades at the next cycle. We pro-rate everything to the day.

  • How long does setup take?

    Most teams are live in under 30 minutes: connect, approve twelve drafts, run a supervised week.

  • Where is my data stored?

    Your choice of US or EU region at signup. It never moves without your written approval.

  • Do you offer refunds?

    First 30 days, no questions. After that, pro-rated to the day you cancel.

  • Does it work with Gmail and Outlook?

    Both, plus any IMAP inbox. Calendar sync covers Google and Microsoft 365.

  • Do you train models on my messages?

    Never. Your messages improve only your own voice profile. Audited annually.
