Is the free plan really free?

Yes — no card, no trial clock, no feature hostage-taking. It's the real product with sane limits.

Will it work with my weird setup?

Gmail, Outlook, any IMAP inbox, plus a REST API for everything else. If it sends email, we can probably read it.

Who sees my messages?

Your team, and nobody else. Support can only view threads you explicitly share with a support link.

Can I talk to a human?

Seven days a week, median reply eleven minutes. The humans are engineers, not a script.
