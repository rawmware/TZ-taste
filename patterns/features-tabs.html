Product tour

01 · Capture

Every message, one queue

Email, chat, and form submissions arrive in a shared inbox. Assign, reply, or snooze — the thread keeps its full history either way.

Boards that sort themselves

Rules route each thread to the right column the moment it arrives. Drag a card when a human disagrees — the rule learns from the correction.

03 · Automate

Follow-ups on rails

Set the trigger — three days of silence, an unpaid invoice, a trial ending — and the message goes out in your voice, on schedule.

04 · Report

Numbers without the spreadsheet

Response times, resolution rates, and revenue per channel — rendered as charts you can actually read, exported in one click.
