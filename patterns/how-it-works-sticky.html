How it works

From first call
to Friday reports.

Phase 01 · Week one

We listen first

A 45-minute call maps how work actually moves through your team — not the org chart, the real paths. We take notes; you talk.

Phase 02 · Week two

We wire it up

Connections, routing rules, and your first templates. You approve a dozen drafts so the system learns your voice, not a generic one.

Phase 03 · Week three

You take the wheel

Drafts keep arriving for approval while you build trust. Flip individual senders to automatic whenever you're ready — or never.

Phase 04 · Ongoing

It compounds

Quarterly reviews tune the rules against your history. Most teams cut triage time in half by month three and keep improving.
