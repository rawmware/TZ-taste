Assembly instructions · read before building

Four steps.
Zero guesswork.

  1. STEP 01← 4 min →

    Connect your tools

    Authorize your inbox and calendar over OAuth. We index your history locally first, then sync — nothing readable leaves your machine until you say so.

  2. STEP 02← 20 min →

    Approve the drafts

    Twelve sample replies land in your queue. Correct them the way you'd correct an intern — the system builds your voice profile from every edit.

  3. STEP 03← 7 days →

    Run supervised

    A full week in dry-run mode: drafts queue up, nothing sends. Review the queue each morning until the approval rate tells you it's ready.

  4. STEP 04← 1 click →

    Release selectively

    Flip senders to automatic one by one. Keep the VIPs on manual approval forever if you like — most teams do.

Tolerances: ±0 surprises · All measurements in honest minutes
