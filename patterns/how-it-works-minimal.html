How it works

  1. 01 — Connect

    Authorize your inbox. Four minutes, one OAuth screen, no credit card.

  2. 02 — Teach

    Approve a dozen drafts. Every correction becomes part of your voice profile.

  3. 03 — Supervise

    One week of dry runs. Drafts queue for review; nothing sends on its own.

  4. 04 — Release

    Move senders to automatic when the approval rate earns your trust.

What you need: an inbox, twenty minutes, and twelve sent emails you're proud of.
