よくある質問 · FAQ

Quiet answers.

Is there a free plan?
Yes. One inbox, a hundred replies a month, no card required. It stays free.
How fast is setup?
Under half an hour. The longest part is approving the twelve training drafts.
Can it write like me?
It learns from your corrections. Most people stop editing drafts within two weeks.
Will it send without asking?
Only for senders you mark automatic — and every send has a ten-minute undo.
Where does my data live?
The region you choose at signup. It never moves without your written say-so.
How do I leave?
Two clicks. Exports free for thirty days, then a confirmed purge.
