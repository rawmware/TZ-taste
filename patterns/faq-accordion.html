FAQ

Asked, answered,
no sales pitch.

Do I need a credit card for the free plan?+

No. The free plan asks for an email and nothing else. We only ask for payment when you choose a paid plan, and the trial never converts silently.

What happens to my data if I cancel?+

It's yours. Export everything as CSV or JSON before or after cancelling — exports stay available for 30 days. Then we purge it, and send you confirmation.

Can it really write in my voice?+

It drafts from your sent history, and it gets better every time you correct it. Most people stop editing drafts after two weeks. Skeptics become believers around day ten.

Will it ever send something without my approval?+

Only for senders you explicitly flip to automatic — and even then, there's a 10-minute undo window on every send. Nothing has ever gone out that a customer didn't authorize.

Do you train AI models on my messages?+

No. Your messages train your voice profile and nothing else. We don't sell data, we don't share it, and our SOC 2 audit checks this specifically.
