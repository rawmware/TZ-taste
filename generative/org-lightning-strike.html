Lightning Strike

A bolt is a line subdivided again and again, each midpoint shoved sideways by a shrinking random offset — the classic midpoint-displacement fractal. Branches spawn recursively from random midpoints, and the whole thing is drawn in three passes: amber wash, bright core, white-hot spine.

One distinctive choice: click anywhere and the bolt drops from the sky to exactly that point. The storm also strikes on its own every few seconds. Seed .
