- `skill/SKILL.md` portable anti-slop skill (VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY dials)
- 12 style DNAs with machine-readable `styles/index.json`
- 14 copy-paste section patterns with `patterns/index.json`
- 6 build prompts (the free prompt library)
- `docs/`: ANTI-SLOP.md checklist, DECISION-MATRIX.md, TASTE-GUIDE.md
- `sources/`: 15 curated free resources, licenses verified
- `scripts/`: freshness engine + validator; weekly CI
- Live demo site ("The Taste Bureau") on GitHub Pages
- `manifest.json`, `llms.txt`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`
