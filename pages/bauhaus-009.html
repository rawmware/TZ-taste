FAQ

Good questions

How big are classes?

Ten max, usually eight. Small enough that the instructor knows your name by week two.

Do I need experience?

No. Every course starts at zero and assumes your hands are smarter than you think.

Are materials included?

Yes — the course fee covers all materials. You take home everything you make.

What if I miss a week?

One catch-up session per course, scheduled with your instructor. Life happens.
