# Italian Futurist

> Italian futurism: speed lines, dynamism, chrome and red.
> A racing poster that refuses to sit still.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f5f1e4` | Aged poster paper |
| `--surface` | `#16130e` | Ink black — burst panels |
| `--ink` | `#16130e` | Near-black type |
| `--muted` | `#7c7568` | Dust gray, small print |
| `--accent` | `#d62b1f` | Racing red — speed lines, one burst per section |
| `--line` | `#16130e26` | Hairline rules (alpha allowed on lines) |

## Type

- **Display:** Archivo Black (uppercase, skewed `-8deg` italic, tight `-0.02em` tracking, sizes that shout)
- **Body:** Archivo (400/500, short sentences, quick rhythm)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (speeds, dates — "240 km/h", "DOM 14")

Google Fonts: `Archivo+Black` `Archivo:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Diagonals everywhere: sections tilt, headlines break the grid, speed lines radiate from the focal point.
- Type layers overlap — a giant background word, a sharp foreground line.
- Radius: `0px`. Speed has no rounded corners.

## Motion

Fast. Elements rush in with a streak (translate + opacity, 300–450ms,
ease-out). A marquee band of repeated words runs the length of the page.
Hovers jolt forward. With reduced motion: a static poster, still loud.

## Do

- Write headlines that move: "VELOCITÀ." / "FASTER THAN THE FERRY."
- Repeat one word three times at three sizes across a section.
- Let one red burst own each section — radiating lines, not decoration.
- Microcopy with a pulse: "Espresso in 20 seconds. No waiting, no small talk."

## Don't

- No centered calm — symmetry is the enemy here.
- No gradients; the red is flat, like poster ink.
- No geometric propaganda grids — that is a different DNA.
- No emoji icons, no lorem. Write it loud or don't write it.

## The one weird thing

One word per page is set absurdly large, tilted, and cropped by the viewport
edge — the page is moving too fast to contain it.

## Best for

Motorsport, espresso brands, sneakers, exhibitions.

`hero-kinetic` `marquee` `features-numbered` `stats-bars` `cta-card` `footers-marquee`

## Lineage

The 1910s–20s Italian futurist manifestos — dynamism over stillness, diagonal
force lines, words set free on the page, the machine age at full throttle.
Not constructivist-red: this is Italian speed — noise, motion, chrome — where
constructivist-red is Russian geometric propaganda — order, geometry, the grid
as ideology. Principles only — 100% original implementation.
