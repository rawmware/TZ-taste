## Type

- **Display:** Archivo — expanded, engineered headlines with wide tracking. Headlines read like instrument labels: confident, uppercase, never decorative.
- **Body:** DM Sans — clean and neutral for findings, field notes, and gear descriptions. The calm narrator under the instruments.
- **Mono:** IBM Plex Mono — coordinates, temperatures, depths, timestamps. Data readouts are the native language of this DNA; give them space and precision.

Google Fonts: `Archivo:wght@500;700;800`, `DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,400;9..40,500`, `IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500;600`

## Spacing & shape

- Modular and grid-snapped: panel-like sections with frost-line borders, aligned like rack equipment.
- Data first: stats and readouts sit in strict columns with consistent baselines.
- Radius minimal (2–4px); instruments are machined, not molded.
- Sections labeled with mono index tags — `SEC.01 / ICE`, `SEC.02 / WIND` — like station modules.

## Motion

Precise and functional: values count up on scroll into view, panels slide in on a straight axis. A slow, subtle scanning line or crosshair drift on the hero evokes instrument sweep without decorating. Motion always means something — a reading updating, a panel opening.

## Do

- Put live-feeling data readouts everywhere: temperature, wind speed, depth, coordinates — real numbers in mono, updated or update-feeling.
- Use the glacier blue strictly for data and interaction; let gray carry the structure.
- Label sections like equipment modules and keep the hierarchy military-clean.
- Show equipment and gear in spec-panel format: image or diagram, then the spec table beside it.

## Don't

- Don't romanticize with moody dark arctic photography — the bright, high-key instrument look is the differentiator.
- Don't use the accent color for decoration — blue means data or action, nothing else.
- Don't round things up or soften the grid — precision is the whole brand.
- Don't write marketing adjectives into the readouts — instruments report, they don't persuade.

## The one weird thing

Add a field-station status bar: a thin fixed strip at the bottom showing live-feeling mono readouts — `TEMP −18.4°C · WIND 42 KM/H · 78.2232°N, 15.6267°E` — static but plausible, updated on an interval with tiny realistic jitter. Or make section headers instrument panels: a bordered plate with the section number, a tiny crosshair SVG, and the title in expanded Archivo. Pick one.
