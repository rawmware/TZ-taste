# Alpine Ledger

> A field logbook stamped at altitude. Kraft paper under everything, entries rubber-stamped like summit certificates, rope and knot linework running along the edges.

## Type

- **Display:** Special Elite — the typewriter voice of the whole site. Headlines read like expedition entries typed at base camp. Use it for all headings, pull quotes, and stamps.
- **Body:** Karla — warm and legible for journal text, trip notes, and packing lists. Handles long paragraphs without going cold.
- **Mono:** IBM Plex Mono — elevation figures, dates, coordinates, gear checklists. Set in small caps where it should feel like instrument readouts.

Google Fonts: `Special Elite`, `Karla:wght@400;600;700`, `IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500`

## Spacing & shape

- Wide margins like a logbook page — content column never runs edge to edge; give it the breathing room of a bound journal.
- Ruled-line rhythm: sections separated by thin ledger rules, occasionally doubled, like ruled paper.
- Corners stay square or softly worn (max 4px radius); nothing pill-shaped survives a mountain.
- Stamps and badges sit slightly rotated (−2° to 2°) so they feel pressed by hand, not aligned by a grid.

## Motion

Motion is analog and restrained: elements fade or stamp in with a quick scale-pop, like a rubber stamp hitting paper. No continuous loops; a stamp landing once on scroll into view is enough. Parallax stays subtle — the mountain does the moving, not the page.

## Do

- Stamp the red accent over headlines, corners, and certificates — "SUMMIT LOGGED", "VERIFIED ROUTE", "DAY 14".
- Use rope or knot linework as section dividers: a simple dashed line reads as a climbing rope at this dialect's scale.
- Give every entry a date + elevation stamp in mono, e.g. `DAY 03 — 3,410 M`.
- Let the paper texture carry the luxury; restrain the palette to kraft, ink, and two accent inks.

## Don't

- Don't use stock alpine photo grids as decoration — one hero plate or none; the paper is the environment.
- Don't set the typewriter font for body text — it fatigues fast and becomes costume instead of voice.
- Don't introduce a clean modern sans for UI chrome — the Karla/IBM Plex Mono pair covers everything.
- Don't overdo the distressing — a little worn edge is character; heavy grunge is theme park.

## The one weird thing

Real logbooks stamp things. Make your primary button a rubber stamp: a bordered mono label in the red accent with a slightly irregular double border, and on click it does the stamp-pop — a fast 110%→100% scale with a faint ink-splat shadow. Or make the section divider a length of knotted rope drawn in SVG with the loop count matching the day count. Pick one.
