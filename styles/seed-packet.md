# Seed Packet

> Engraved print ephemera: kraft paper, copperplate vegetables,
> and planting instructions set like they matter — because they do.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#efe6d0` | Packet kraft |
| `--surface` | `#e3d4b4` | Darker kraft — planting-table bands |
| `--ink` | `#2b2419` | Engraving ink |
| `--muted` | `#7a6c52` | Faded packet back, secondary text |
| `--accent` | `#3f7043` | Packet green — the variety name, one per page |
| `--line` | `#2b24191f` | Engraving-line hairlines (alpha allowed on lines) |

## Type

- **Display:** Fraunces (engraved serif, old-style numerals — "TOMATO · BRANDYWINE", 2–5rem)
- **Body:** Spectral (1.05rem, packet-back instructions with room to breathe)
- **Mono:** Courier Prime (sowing directions — "SOW: AFTER LAST FROST · 1/4 IN")

Google Fonts: `Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144,400;9..144,600;9..144,900` `Spectral:wght@400;500;700` `Courier+Prime:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Packet-front composition: variety name huge, engraving-style illustration,
  planting grid below like the back of the packet.
- Planting tables banded in the darker kraft tone, dense and honest.
- Engraving-line hairlines separate everything; the page feels printed.
- Radius: `2px`. A folded paper edge.

## Motion

Almost none — print does not move. The germination stamp lands once
(opacity only) and sits for the season.
With reduced motion: fully still.

## Do

- Write the packet back like a human: "Sow after the last frost. Thin to twelve inches. Water like you mean it."
- Set real planting data in mono — days to germination, spacing, depth.
- Spend the packet green once per viewport: the variety name or the CTA.
- Microcopy from the seed house: "Packed for this season. 87% germination, tested January."

## Don't

- No purple or blue gradients — packet kraft is flat and printed, never a gradient.
- No garden kitsch: no cartoon sprouts with faces, no clip-art watering cans.
- No emoji icons; no lorem — the packet back has real instructions.
- No lab-white sterile sections. This is a seed house, not a laboratory.

## The one weird thing

A rubber-stamp block — "PACKED FOR 2026 · 87% GERMINATION" — stamped at a
slight angle over the hero in mono, green ink, a little uneven at the edges.
Every page gets its own stamp, like the batch it came from.
