# Library Oak

> Leather and brass: card catalogs, reading lamps, the hush of old oak stacks.
> The page whispers. That is the whole trick.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#211810` | Dark oak background |
| `--surface` | `#2d2318` | Reading-room panels, catalog cards |
| `--ink` | `#ece0c8` | Lamp-lit parchment text |
| `--muted` | `#93805f` | Aged leather — captions, spines, asides |
| `--accent` | `#c9973f` | Brass — call numbers, rules, one lamp-glow detail |
| `--line` | `#c9973f40` | Brass hairline rules (alpha allowed on lines) |

## Type

- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond (bookish, large, sentence case — never shouted)
- **Body:** EB Garamond (1–1.125rem, 55–70 characters per line; the measure of a good page)
- **Mono:** Roboto Mono (call numbers, catalog data — "FIC ROW · 813.54")

Google Fonts: `Cormorant+Garamond:wght@400;600` `EB+Garamond:wght@400;500`
`Roboto+Mono:wght@400;500`

## Spacing & shape

- Calm and centered; generous margins like the borders of a well-set page.
- Radius: `3px` — the soft corner of a catalog card, barely there.
- Brass hairlines separate sections; wide oak-dark space holds them apart.
- Rhythm: long quiet stretches of text, then one brass-ruled card. The hush does the work.

## Motion

Almost none, on purpose. Cards fade in over 500ms; the brass rule under a
headline draws itself once, slowly. A library does not animate — it turns
pages. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`: everything is simply already there.

## Do

- Style section headers as card-catalog cards: mono "SUBJECT: … / AUTHOR: … / CALL NO: …" above each display headline.
- Let brass do the ornament: one rule, one call number, one lamp-glow per section.
- Write directions like a librarian would: "Fiction A–C · third stack on the left."
- Keep the humor dry and real: "Due back Tuesday. No fines — just guilt."

## Don't

- No glass cases, no didactic wall labels — books are for reading, not viewing.
- No gradients, no neon; lamplight is warm and flat.
- No three-equal-cards of "collections" — shelves are lists, ordered and calm.
- No lorem — a library page with fake titles is an empty shelf. Name the actual books.

## The one weird thing

Add a "recently returned" shelf: a single horizontal row of mono spine labels
("ROW 813.54 — GARCÍA MÁRQUEZ, G.", "ROW 823.91 — WOOLF, V."), small brass
rules between them, like the cart by the returns desk. One shelf per page —
the rest stays hushed.

## Best for

Bookstores, publishers, archives, universities.

## Lineage

Oak-paneled reading rooms and card catalogs; brass reading-lamp light on
parchment; the catalog card as the original structured data — subject,
author, call number, nothing else. Not museum-placard: the museum puts
objects behind glass and labels them from a distance; the library hands you
the book — this copy invites reading, not viewing. Circulation, not
exhibition. Principles only — 100% original implementation.
