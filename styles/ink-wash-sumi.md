# Ink-Wash Sumi

> Brush, ink, rice paper: monochrome gestures and the confidence to leave space alone.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f5f1e8` | Rice paper |
| `--surface` | `#efe9db` | Paper panel |
| `--ink` | `#1a1a1a` | Full-strength ink |
| `--muted` | `#8a8478` | Dilute ink wash |
| `--accent` | `#a33b32` | Seal red — used like a hanko stamp, rarely |
| `--line` | `#d8d0bd` | Faintest wash edge |

## Type

- **Display:** Zen Old Mincho, large, set with huge leading — one or two lines per section, like an inscription
- **Body:** Zen Kaku Gothic New 400, quiet and even
- **Mono:** Space Mono, only where numbers must be precise

Google Fonts: `Zen+Old+Mincho:wght@400;700` `Zen+Kaku+Gothic+New:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Vast negative space — content floats like a single bamboo stalk on paper.
- Vertical rhythm over boxes: no cards, no panels; rules are brush strokes (SVG, varying width).
- Text columns narrow and off-center; the asymmetry does the framing.
- Radius: `0`. Paper has edges; so does everything on it.

## Motion

Barely there. Slow fade-ins like ink drying, brush-stroke underlines that draw
once per visit, parallax at walking pace. If a user notices the motion, there's
too much of it.

## Do

- One gestural brush SVG per section — a stroke, an ensō circle, a horizon wash
- Wash gradients in gray only (never color gradients), from ink to paper
- Seal-red reserved for one mark: the CTA, the logo, the stamp. One.
- Microcopy in short lines: "Brew. Sit. The tea is enough."

## Don't

- No cards, no boxes, no containers — this DNA forbids the rectangle habit
- No photography without a heavy monochrome wash treatment
- No second accent color — seal red is the only warmth allowed
- No crowded pages: if density feels above 1, remove something

## The one weird thing

Every page gets exactly one brush moment: either the hero headline is underscored
by a single hand-drawn stroke that loads once, or the whole page's section
breaks are one continuous scroll-drawing line. One stroke, never both. Pick one.

## Best for

Tea brands, galleries, calligraphy, meditation apps, craft studios.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `nav-minimal`, `testimonials`, `cta`, `footer`

## Lineage

East Asian brush painting: the economy of a single gesture, and the discipline
of knowing the blank paper is already part of the picture.
