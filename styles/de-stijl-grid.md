# De Stijl Grid

> Mondrian's canvas as a website: primary blocks, black grids, white doing the heavy lifting.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#faf8f4` | Canvas white |
| `--surface` | `#ffffff` | Block white |
| `--ink` | `#111111` | Grid black |
| `--muted` | `#8c877e` | Caption gray |
| `--accent` | `#e3002b` | Primary red — the one large block |
| `--accent-2` | `#0057a8` | Primary blue — sparing |
| `--line` | `#111111` | Black grid lines, 8px minimum |

## Type

- **Display:** Archivo Black, uppercase, blocky — sits inside red or black blocks like a label
- **Body:** Archivo 400/500, left-aligned, short paragraphs in white rectangles
- **Mono:** Space Mono for captions, dates, coordinates of the work

Google Fonts: `Archivo+Black` `Archivo:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- The grid is the design: black rules 8px+ divide the page into unequal rectangles.
- Asymmetry is law — never center, never equal columns, never symmetrical thirds.
- Color blocks carry function: red = the headline block, blue/yellow = accents only, once each.
- Radius: `0`. Rectangle purity.

## Motion

Almost none. Blocks shift on hover with a single crisp step; page transitions
are hard cuts. A grid this disciplined doesn't animate — it asserts.

## Do

- Treat each page section as a composition: balance red mass against white void
- Let white space dominate — the emptiness is the composition
- Use black bars as structural rules between every major section
- Label everything like a gallery plate: title, year, medium, dimensions

## Don't

- No curves, no shadows, no gradients — plastic purity
- No equal columns and no centered text — that's decoration, not neoplasticism
- No more than three colored blocks per page — restraint is the style
- No functional-school literalism — this is painting, not the `swiss-rational` poster

## The one weird thing

One block breaks the grid. Either a single rectangle overflows its border by a
fixed 24px, or one section inverts to black-on-white. The violation is the
signature — but there can only be one. Pick one.

## Best for

Galleries, design studios, book publishers, exhibition sites, portfolios.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `nav-minimal`, `stats`, `footer`, `background-grain`

## Lineage

Neoplasticist painting: reducing a composition to primary relationships of
color, line, and white space — and trusting the white to do the work.
