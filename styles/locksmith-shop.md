# Locksmith Shop

> Brass and steel: the key machine hums, the ticket book never lies,
> and every key leaves with a story the customer almost tells you.

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#ece7db` | Shop-counter paper |
| `--surface` | `#ded6c2` | Darker counter — ticket bands |
| `--ink` | `#26221a` | Stencil ink |
| `--muted` | `#7d7461` | Brass tarnish, secondary text |

## Type

- **Display:** Big Shoulders Stencil Text (shop signage, key-blank numbers — "SC1", uppercase)
- **Body:** Karla (1rem, plainspoken counter talk)
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono (tickets and blanks — "Ticket #1042 · SC1 · $8")

Google Fonts: `Big+Shoulders+Stencil+Text:wght@400;700` `Karla:wght@400;500;700` `JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500`

## Spacing & shape

- Ticket-counter density: honest rows of services, each with a price in mono.
- Key-blank charts in a tight grid, like the wall behind the counter.
- Stencil rules separate sections; nothing floats without a reason.
- Radius: `2px`. Stamped, not rounded.

## Motion

A stamp effect on the headline — it lands once (opacity + slight scale via
transform), like the ticket being punched. Everything else stays put.
With reduced motion: the stamp is already stamped.

## Do

- Talk like the counter: "Locked out? Bring the lock. We'll sort it."
- Price everything in mono — a locksmith's prices are public and proud.
- Spend the cut brass once per viewport: the key-blank chart or the CTA.
- Microcopy from the shop: "We copy keys. We do not copy excuses."

## Don't

- No purple or blue gradients — the shop is flat paper and brass, never a gradient.
- No spy-movie kitsch: no lockpicks crossed like swords, no trench coats.
- No emoji icons; no lorem — the ticket book has real numbers.
- No fake urgency ("24/7 EMERGENCY!!!" in red). State the hours and mean them.

## The one weird thing

A punched ticket stub pinned in the corner — "KEEP THIS TICKET · #1042" —
with a perforated edge, in mono. The stub number increments on every visit,
like the shop is actually open.
