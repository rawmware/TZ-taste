# Nordic Noir

> Overcast crime scene at noon. Desaturated slate, hard facts, no comfort.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#dfe3e6` | Case-file gray — cold and even |
| `--surface` | `#f1f3f5` | Evidence-white — cards, panels |
| `--ink` | `#131a24` | Near-black blue — headlines, body |
| `--muted` | `#7d8994` | Fog — captions, timestamps |
| `--accent` | `#3f647f` | Police-blue, washed out — emphasis, CTAs |
| `--line` | `#b9c1c9` | Rules, tables, redaction boxes |

## Type

- **Display:** Bebas Neue — tall, cold, accusatory headlines. All caps always;
  it does the interrogation for you.
- **Body:** DM Sans 400 — deliberately plain; the plainness is the point.
- **Mono:** IBM Plex Mono — case numbers, dates, times, coordinates
  (`CASE 07-118 — 04:12 — 62.39° N`).

Google Fonts: `Bebas+Neue&family=DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,400;9..40,500&family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500`

## Spacing & shape

- Evidence-board rhythm: strict columns, index numbers in the margin, long
  horizontal rules like folder dividers.
- Redaction-style blocks (`█` and solid bars) as graphic texture — used as
  dividers, never hiding real content.
- Radius: `0` everywhere. Corners are suspects too.
- Dense vertical rhythm: lines close together, sections separated by thick
  rules, not air.

## Motion

Subtle and forensic: a slow horizontal scan-line on hero imagery, case cards
that snap into place with a hard cut. No easing curves that feel warm.
Everything moves like it's on a timetable.

## Do

- Margin case numbers (`01`, `02`) running down the page like a file index
- Short declarative headlines: "She never called back." Let the cold do it.
- Timeline layouts with mono timestamps
- One desaturated photograph per page, treated almost gray

## Don't

- No warm colors anywhere — no amber, no red CTAs
- No rounded corners, no soft shadows, no friendly microcopy
- No more than one accent usage per viewport; restraint is the whole aesthetic
- No exclamation marks. Ever.

## The one weird thing

Either every date is stamped like an evidence tag, or each section opens with
a location line ("MALMÖ — FEBRUARY — 3°C"). Pick one.

## Best for

Streaming platforms, publishers, true-crime podcasts, documentary series,
mystery games.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-kinetic`, `stats`, `testimonials`, `marquee`, `background-grain`

## Lineage

The forensic patience of Scandinavian crime-series title sequences; the
document layout of an actual case file, where the form is the drama.
