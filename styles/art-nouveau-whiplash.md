# Art Nouveau Whiplash

> Mucha posters and ironwork: organic curves, jewel tones, every frame an ornament.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#1c1a16` | Aged gallery wall |
| `--surface` | `#262219` | Framed panel |
| `--ink` | `#ece2cd` | Ivory paper |
| `--muted` | `#8a7a5e` | Faded bronze |
| `--accent` | `#c9a227` | Antique gold — rules, initials, frames |
| `--accent-2` | `#4e7d6a` | Muted teal — jewel tone contrast |
| `--line` | `#3a342a` | Hairline frames |

## Type

- **Display:** Cinzel Decorative, title case, `clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 6rem)`, letter-spaced like a playbill
- **Body:** Cormorant Garamond 400/italic, generous leading — read like a program note
- **Mono:** Space Mono, but only for dates, times, and seat numbers

Google Fonts: `Cinzel+Decorative:wght@400;700` `Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;1,400` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Ornaments earn their place: borders are the layout, not the decoration.
- Wide margins — artwork breathes like it's hung in a gallery.
- Asymmetric frames: content drifts off-center inside curved borders.
- Hairlines (`1px`) for frames; never thick boxes.

## Motion

Slow and ceremonial. Reveal-on-scroll fades with slight upward drift, ornamental
divs that draw themselves in, no bounces, no springs. Motion here is a curtain
rising, not a button popping.

## Do

- Decorative SVG flourishes between sections (whiplash curves, botanical borders)
- Drop caps and decorated initials at the start of key passages
- Muted jewel tones (deep teal, wine, moss) against the aged-dark background
- Microcopy with theater etiquette: "Seats are limited. Reserve yours."

## Don't

- No sans-serif headlines — the moment you go Helvetica, the spell breaks
- No flat minimalism masquerading as elegance; ornament is the point
- No neon, no high saturation — jewel tones are deep, never loud
- No stock photos of "luxury" — illustration or nothing

## The one weird thing

Give one element the full treatment. Either the hero headline gets the
illuminated-manuscript treatment (flourished cap, gold rules) or every section
divider becomes a hand-drawn botanical frame. One jewel, polished; the rest
quiet. Pick one.

## Best for

Theaters, wineries, spas, concert halls, artisan brands.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `nav-minimal`, `testimonials`, `cta`, `footer`

## Lineage

The whiplash line of poster lithography; the framing instinct of Victorian
playbills — ornament that carries meaning instead of hiding it.
