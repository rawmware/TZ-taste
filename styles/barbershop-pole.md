## Type

- **Display:** Alfa Slab One (painted window lettering — "CUTS", "SHAVE", 3–5rem)
- **Body:** Source Sans 3 (1rem, plainspoken, the price list voice)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (hours, dates — "TUE–SAT 9–6", "EST. 1987")

Google Fonts: `Alfa+Slab+One` `Source+Sans+3:wght@400;600;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- The service menu is a real price list: items left, dotted leaders, prices right.
- A thin vertical barber stripe (static, red/ink) marks the active section edge.
- Brass-rule dividers — two thin lines — separate major sections.
- Radius: `0px`. Tile doesn't round.

## Motion

Headline underlines draw in with scaleX (transform only). The mini pole beside
the nav rotates a quarter turn as you pass each section. With reduced motion:
the pole stands still.

## Do

- Set the menu like the window: "Cut .... $30", "Hot shave .... $25", leaders and all.
- Write copy the shop would actually say: "No appointment? Come anyway. We'll fit you in."
- Spend the barber red once per viewport: the pole or the CTA, never both.
- Keep hours and prices where a passerby can read them without scrolling.

## Don't

- No purple or blue gradients — the shop is tile-white and red, never a glow.
- No cartoon mustache icons, no clipart scissors, nothing cute.
- No stock barber photo with a dark overlay; the price list is the hero.
- No emoji, no lorem — the shop keeps real hours.

## The one weird thing

The section nav is a mini barber pole — a thin vertical striped bar beside the
headings that rotates a quarter turn as you scroll past each section, like the
real one outside the door.
