# Editorial Serif

> Warm paper, oversized serif, hairline rules. A literary magazine that happens
> to be a website.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f5f1e8` | Warm paper background |
| `--surface` | `#efe9da` | Cards, inset panels |
| `--ink` | `#1c1a15` | Primary text |
| `--muted` | `#6f6a5e` | Secondary text, captions |
| `--accent` | `#b5461f` | Burnt sienna — links, highlights, one graphic per page |
| `--line` | `#1c1a1526` | Hairline rules |

## Type

- **Display:** Fraunces (72pt optical sizing, `-0.03em` tracking, italic for emphasis words)
- **Body:** Newsreader (1.05–1.15rem, 1.65 line-height, 60ch measure)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (eyebrows, labels, captions — uppercase, `0.14em` tracking)

Google Fonts: `Fraunces:ital,opsz,wght@0,9..144,300..700;1,9..144,300..700`
`Newsreader:opsz,wght@6..72,400..500` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Rhythm: `24 / 48 / 96 / 160` px section padding. Never cramped.
- Radius: `2px` max. This DNA does not do pill buttons — rectangular or
  underline links.
- Rules: `1px` hairlines separate everything. Borders are the grid.

## Motion

Subtle. Fade + `translateY(12px)` entrances, `1.1` image scale on hover.
No bouncy springs — this DNA glides.

## Do

- Drop caps on lead paragraphs, oversized numerals for section indices (`01`)
- Pull quotes set in Fraunces italic at 2x body size
- One focal graphic per page: an arch image, a duotone portrait, a single chart
- Footnotes and marginalia — editorial texture

## Don't

- No gradients, no glass, no shadows deeper than `0 1px 0`
- No centered hero with three cards (banned globally, doubly here)
- No more than one accent-colored element per viewport

## The one weird thing

A rotated marginal note, a giant section numeral bleeding off the edge, or a
single word set 4x larger mid-paragraph. Pick one.

## Best for

Studios, blogs, portfolios, publishers, anyone selling judgment.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `testimonials`, `footer`, `marquee`
