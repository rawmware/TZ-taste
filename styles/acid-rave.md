# Acid Rave

> Black room, acid lime, type at maximum volume. Flyers, not landing pages.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0a0a0a` | Black room |
| `--surface` | `#131313` | Slightly lifted black |
| `--ink` | `#f2f2f2` | Off-white type |
| `--muted` | `#7a7a7a` | Secondary |
| `--accent` | `#c6ff00` | Acid lime — headlines, highlights, marquees |
| `--line` | `#f2f2f21f` | Faint white rules |

## Type

- **Display:** Anton, uppercase, `clamp(4rem, 14vw, 12rem)`, `-0.01em` — maximum volume
- **Body:** Space Grotesk 400/500, short lines
- **Mono:** Space Mono for dates, venues, lineups

Google Fonts: `Anton` `Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Type IS the layout. Massive headlines, minimal chrome.
- Radius: `0`. Marquees separate sections.
- Overlapping, rotated, cropped — controlled chaos.

## Motion

High energy: marquees, strobing hovers (respect reduced-motion!), scroll-driven
type scaling, glitch on interaction. If it feels safe, push further.

## Do

- Lineup/dates as typographic posters
- Acid highlight behind key words (`background: accent; color: black`)
- Grain overlay, scanline textures
- Ticket/CTA buttons in solid acid with black text

## Don't

- No pastels, no rounded cards, no polite spacing
- No long paragraphs — this DNA communicates in shouts and labels
- No photography without heavy treatment (duotone, high contrast)

## The one weird thing

This DNA *is* the weird thing. But still: one moment of restraint (a quiet
ticket box, a calm footer) makes the loud parts louder.

## Best for

Music, events, streetwear, festivals, nightlife brands.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-kinetic`, `marquee`, `cta`, `background-grain`
