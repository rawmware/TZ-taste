# Botanical Lab

> A greenhouse full of specimens: deep green, pressed-plant delicacy, Latin
> on every label.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f4f6ec` | Greenhouse paper — pale leaf-tint |
| `--surface` | `#fbfdf7` | Specimen card — clean white-green |
| `--ink` | `#1b3a2b` | Deep green — text |
| `--muted` | `#6f867a` | Moss — captions, secondary text |

## Type

- **Display:** DM Serif Display 400 — soft authority; headlines feel like a
  well-kept herbarium label, writ large.
- **Body:** Karla 400 — friendly and plainspoken; ingredient stories live here.
- **Mono:** Space Mono — Latin names, batch numbers, harvest dates
  (`CAMELLIA SINENSIS — BATCH 114 — APR 2026`).

Google Fonts: `DM+Serif+Display:ital@0;1&family=Karla:wght@400;500;700&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Specimen-card layout: thin rules, Latin name above the common name, batch
  number in the corner — the card format repeats across products and sections.
- Delicate line illustrations of leaves/veins as dividers, not photos of
  textures.
- Radius: `12px` on cards (soft, organic), `0` on rule boxes.
- Airy sections with one botanical detail per viewport — restraint, not jungle.

## Motion

Gentle: leaves sway a few degrees on scroll, specimen cards lift softly on
hover. Think greenhouse, not storm. Transitions are slow fades, never snaps.

## Do

- Latin name in italic serif above every product or ingredient name
- Batch/harvest lines in mono under every card
- Line-drawn botanical dividers between sections
- Ingredient lists written like field notes: plain, specific, no hype

## Don't

- No neon or saturated greens — this is soil, not a rave
- No stock "wellness" copy; say what's in it and where it grew
- No cluttered grids; specimens get space like they do on the herbarium sheet
- No dark forest backgrounds; the palette stays light and breathable

## The one weird thing

Either a "today in the greenhouse" note — a small dated observation in mono
at the top of each page — or every product card carries a pressed-leaf detail
image. Pick one.

## Best for

Skincare, tea brands, garden centers, apothecaries, seed companies.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `cards`, `nav-minimal`, `stats`, `footer`

## Lineage

The labeling discipline of a herbarium sheet — every specimen identified,
dated, and placed with care; the typographic calm of old seed packets.
