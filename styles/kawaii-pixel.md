# Kawaii Pixel

> Sticker-sheet joy: pastel pixels, chunky hearts, everything huggable at 8x scale.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#fff7f9` | Blush paper |
| `--surface` | `#ffeef4` | Sticker back |
| `--ink` | `#4a3340` | Cocoa ink |
| `--muted` | `#b09aa4` | Soft gray-pink |
| `--accent` | `#ff8fb3` | Sakura pink — buttons, hearts, highlights |
| `--accent-2` | `#7dd8c6` | Mint — the fresh contrast |
| `--line` | `#f3d9e2` | Pastel borders |

## Type

- **Display:** DotGothic16, large — chunky pixel letterforms softened by the pastel palette
- **Body:** M PLUS Rounded 1c 400/700, rounded and warm
- **Mono:** Space Mono, used playfully: prices on sticker labels, "LVL 3" badges

Google Fonts: `DotGothic16` `M+PLUS+Rounded+1c:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Sticker logic: chunky white-bordered elements that look peelable, offset at playful angles.
- Radius on non-pixel elements: big and soft (`16px–24px`); pixel elements stay square.
- Generous pastel padding — the page feels like the inside of a stationery shop.
- Two pixel scales: small pixels for icons, giant pixels for hero moments.

## Motion

Bounce everywhere (with reduced-motion respect): springy button squishes,
wiggling stickers on hover, pixel-heart particles on the CTA, confetti that's
actually little squares. Joy is the animation budget.

## Do

- Pixel art icons: hearts, stars, cats, onigiri — chunky and proud
- Sticker-sheet sectioning: elements arranged like they could be peeled off
- Mint + pink pairing as the signature two-color rhythm
- Microcopy with shop-counter warmth: "Made fresh this morning!"

## Don't

- No darkness — this is the pastel twin of `pixel-arcade`; never borrow its night
- No sharp corporate minimalism; softness is structural here
- No desaturation: pastels are gentle but the pinks must still read as pink
- No sarcasm in the copy — this DNA is sincere

## The one weird thing

Choose the sticker. Either one hero pixel character (a cat, a ghost, a blob)
appears everywhere like a mascot, or the whole page uses a single repeating
pixel motif (hearts, stars) as its connective tissue. One character or one
motif. Pick one.

## Best for

Stationery, cafes, indie games, bakeries, kids' brands, craft shops.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-kinetic`, `cards`, `marquee`, `cta`, `nav-pill`

## Lineage

Japanese stationery culture and sticker sheets: the craft of making something
small feel precious, and the confidence that cuteness is a design system.
