# Surf Shack

> Sun-bleached and hand-painted: faded teal, warm sand, and signage you can
> tell a person made.

## Type

- **Display:** Shrikhand 400 — hand-painted sign energy; headlines read like
  the board outside the shop. Set big, slightly tilted when it helps.
- **Body:** DM Sans 400 — easy, plain, friendly. No one's in a hurry here.
- **Mono:** Space Mono — hours, tide times, board sizes
  (`OPEN 8–6 — HIGH TIDE 11:42 — 7'2"`).

Google Fonts: `Shrikhand&family=DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,400;9..40,500;9..40,700&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Painted-sign composition: wavy dividers, plank-like horizontal bands,
  arrow-shaped CTAs that point where you should look.
- Hand-drawn borders (slightly wobbly SVG strokes) on cards and menu boxes.
- Sun-fade texture: subtle noise and soft-edged color fields, like paint left
  in the sun.
- Radius: wavy, irregular — use `border-radius` with uneven values or hand-drawn
  SVG frames.

## Motion

Easy and buoyant: sections drift up like they're floating, signs sway gently,
the wave divider undulates slowly. CTAs wobble on hover like a hanging sign in
the breeze. Nothing urgent — it's always almost sunset.

## Do

- Shrikhand headlines over sand, one word in teal or coral per line
- Hand-drawn wavy dividers between sections
- Menu-board layouts with mono hours and prices
- Stamps and stickers: "Local", "Since '94", "Cash only" — rotated, worn

## Don't

- No corporate polish: if it looks like a template, sand it down
- No navy-and-white "yacht club" minimalism — this is a shack, not a resort
- No long paragraphs; sign copy is short or it's ignored
- No dark sections; the sun never goes down here

## The one weird thing

Either a daily conditions strip ("SWELL 3FT — WIND OFFSHORE — 68°F") running
in mono across the top of every page, or every CTA is a hand-drawn arrow
pointing somewhere. Pick one.

## Best for

Surf shops, beach cafes, travel brands, hostels, board rentals.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `marquee`, `cta`, `cards`, `background-grain`

## Lineage

The vernacular craft of hand-painted shop signage — every letter earned;
the color memory of faded boardshorts and sun-hit paint, never neon, never new.
