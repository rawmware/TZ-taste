# Gothic Editorial

> Dark academia: candlelit paper, blackletter accents used sparingly,
> scholarly and brooding. A library after hours.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#12100c` | Candlelit dark, near-black with warmth |
| `--surface` | `#1b1712` | Panels, chapter cards, pull-quotes |
| `--ink` | `#e8dfc8` | Aged paper text |
| `--muted` | `#7d7261` | Faded ink, marginalia, captions |

## Type

- **Display:** UnifrakturMaguntia (blackletter — SPARING: one headline or initial per section, never paragraphs)
- **Body:** Cormorant Garamond (400/500, 1.1rem, 1.7 line-height — the reading voice, literary and unhurried)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (catalog numbers, footnotes, archival labels)

Google Fonts: `UnifrakturMaguntia` `Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;1,400`
`Space+Mono:wght@400`

## Spacing & shape

- Manuscript layout: wide margins, centered column, generous top whitespace — the page as a codex.
- Ornaments as structure: thin rules, fleuron-like separators, framed initials.
- Radius: `0px` on frames; imagery in sharp rectangles with dark borders.
- Rhythm: chapter-like. Big pauses, then dense columns of Cormorant. Number your sections like chapters.

## Motion

Solemn and slow. Fades like candlelight (800ms+), drop caps that reveal
gracefully, pages that turn with weight. Hover states deepen color rather than
moving. Motion should feel ceremonial — if it entertains, it's too fast.

## Do

- Use UnifrakturMaguntia for ONE display moment per section — a chapter number, a pull-quote initial, the hero word. Scarcity is its power.
- Number sections like chapters ("Chapter I", "Chapter II") with oxblood numerals.
- Write in a scholarly but human voice: "We found this in the stacks.", footnotes that actually say things.
- Let dark negative space dominate — the library is mostly quiet shelves.

## Don't

- No blackletter for body copy or UI — unreadable and the fastest way to costume territory.
- No purple, no neon, no modern tech sheen; the palette is paper, ink, oxblood, dark wood.
- No horror tropes (blood drips, bats, jagged edges) — this is academia, not Halloween.
- No bright white backgrounds; the light here comes from candles, not fluorescents.

## The one weird thing

Add one archival detail per page: a marginal note in the sidebar ("see also:
folio 44"), a footnote with a real sentence in it, or an "ex libris" style
colophon mark. Scholarship lives in the margins — one marginalia element makes
the page feel researched rather than decorated. Pick one.
