# Risograph Grain

> Misregistered zine ink on cream: teal over pink, grain you can feel,
> handmade edges.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f5eedd` | Uncoated cream stock |
| `--surface` | `#fbf6e9` | Cleaner sheet — cards |
| `--ink` | `#26413c` | Deep teal-ink — text |
| `--muted` | `#8d8a76` | Faded ink — captions |
| `--accent` | `#117d78` | Soy teal — headlines, fills |
| `--accent-2` | `#ef6fa5` | Fluorescent pink — overprints, highlights |
| `--line` | `#d8cfae` | Paper-edge rules |

## Type

- **Display:** Archivo Black 400 — set in teal, often slightly offset from its
  own pink ghost copy for the misregistration look.
- **Body:** Space Grotesk 400 — readable and a little rough around the edges.
- **Mono:** Space Mono — edition numbers, print dates, colophon details
  (`ED. 200 — 2 COLORS — 2026`).

Google Fonts: `Archivo+Black&family=Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;700&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Offset duplicates: headlines printed twice, the second shifted 2–4px in
  pink underneath teal — that's the misregistration signature.
- Grain overlay everywhere (`SVG noise`, low opacity) — the texture is
  non-negotiable.
- Slight rotations (±1deg) on images and pull quotes, like pages fed by hand.
- Radius: `0`. Paper is cut square.

## Motion

Almost none — print doesn't move. A slow grain shimmer on hover, or a headline
that "registers" (pink copy slides into place behind the teal) once on load.
That's the whole show.

## Do

- Two-color palette discipline: teal and pink on cream, nothing else
- Grain overlay at 6–10% opacity over the full page
- Offset-printed headlines (teal over shifted pink)
- Colophon-style footers: edition, colors, paper, "printed at home"

## Don't

- No smooth gradients, no drop shadows, no glass anything
- No third color — two inks, one paper, that's the medium
- No pixel-perfect alignment; if everything lines up exactly, it's not riso
- No photography without a grain + two-color treatment

## The one weird thing

Either every image gets the teal-pink duotone treatment, or the site carries
a running "ink status" footer (`TEAL: 62% — PINK: 38%`). Pick one.

## Best for

Zines, indie presses, poster shops, DIY labels, print studios.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `cards`, `background-grain`, `marquee`, `footer`

## Lineage

The honesty of small-run print shops — two inks, one drum, visible grain;
the layout logic of photocopied zines where the paper and the process are the
style.
