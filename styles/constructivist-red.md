# Constructivist Red

> Propaganda poster as interface: red and black diagonals, type that shouts from a wall.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f2ede4` | Aged paper |
| `--surface` | `#e6ded0` | Poster card |
| `--ink` | `#141414` | Poster black |
| `--muted` | `#6e675c` | Weathered print |
| `--accent` | `#d4201e` | Agitation red — diagonals, emphasis, action |
| `--accent-2` | `#141414` | Black masses — anchors and blocks |
| `--line` | `#141414` | Thick black rules |

## Type

- **Display:** Russo One, uppercase, `clamp(3rem, 10vw, 9rem)` — set on the diagonal or cropped by the viewport edge
- **Body:** Oswald 400, tight, all-caps subheads; sentence case for longer copy
- **Mono:** Space Mono for dates, figures, statistics

Google Fonts: `Russo+One` `Oswald:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Diagonals divide the page — content sits inside tilted bands, sections cut at 12–24 degrees.
- Radius: `0`. Shapes are bars, triangles, circles; nothing soft.
- Overlapping type and geometry: text collides with shapes on purpose.
- Black masses anchor; red cuts through; paper shows everywhere else.

## Motion

March, don't glide. Staggered hard cuts on scroll entry (steps, not easing),
tilting marquee ribbons, red bars that slam in on hover. Kinetic but never bouncy.

## Do

- Photomontage energy: high-contrast duotone imagery, hard crops, silhouettes
- Big figures and slogans — one number or one word per section
- Red diagonal ribbons as the recurring motif (headers, dividers, CTAs)
- Solid red CTA blocks with black text, borders like strike posters

## Don't

- No soft gray SaaS energy — this is agitation, not onboarding
- No subtlety in the palette: red, black, paper. A fourth color is a betrayal
- No rounded corners, no soft shadows, no gentle anything
- No factory-machine literalism — this is poster agitation, not the `industrial-brutalist` factory floor

## The one weird thing

Every page needs one giant crop: a face, a fist, a gear — cut by the viewport
edge at poster scale, monochrome, unavoidable. Either the hero IS the crop or a
section transition is. One crop, poster-size. Pick one.

## Best for

Campaigns, unions, exhibitions, protest design, political orgs, zines.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-kinetic`, `marquee`, `stats`, `cta`, `background-grain`

## Lineage

The agitation-poster tradition of the 1920s: diagonal energy, flat color
masses, type as a physical object on the page.
