# Cottage Warm

> Cozy domestic: cream, checks, garden warmth.
> The website equivalent of someone offering you tea.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#fbf5ea` | Cream paper, warm and unbleached |
| `--surface` | `#f3ebdb` | Linen panels, recipe cards |
| `--ink` | `#3d3229` | Warm brown-black text |
| `--muted` | `#8d7f6e` | Soft taupe, captions and notes |

## Type

- **Display:** DM Serif Display (friendly serif, sentence case — never shouty, never precious)
- **Body:** Karla (400/700, 1.05rem, 1.65 line-height — readable as a handwritten note's clean cousin)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (rare — recipe quantities, dates, quantities only)

Google Fonts: `DM+Serif+Display` `Karla:wght@400;700`
`Space+Mono:wght@400`

## Spacing & shape

- Comfortable and slightly informal: content blocks sit like items on a kitchen table.
- Radius: `10–14px` soft rounds, plus dashed or stitched borders as accents.
- Checks and gingham patterns as texture bands — tablecloth energy, used in narrow strips.
- Rhythm: unhurried. Generous padding, one warm detail per section, no crowding.

## Motion

Gentle and domestic. Soft fades, slight rises (300–500ms, easy ease). Hover
states warm up — a button deepens like toast browning. Paper textures shift
barely. Nothing slides far, nothing spins. The page should feel tended, like a
garden: motion is growth, not performance.

## Do

- Use stitched/dashed borders and check patterns as your signature textures.
- Write copy in the voice of a warm host: "Baked this morning.", "Come hungry.", first names everywhere.
- Photograph things like a home: warm light, wooden surfaces, slightly imperfect styling.
- Let terracotta be the appetite color — food, buttons, the one warm underline per section.

## Don't

- No cold grays, no steel, no minimal-white sterility — warmth is the whole point.
- No harsh geometric brutalism; edges are soft or stitched, never razor.
- No corporate formality ("Solutions", "Leverage") — talk like a person who lives there.
- No more than two accent colors per page: terracotta and garden green is the full pantry.

## The one weird thing

Add one domestic detail per page: a recipe card with actual quantities, a
"from our kitchen" stamp, or a seasonal note ("Made with October apples").
Cottage brands prove care through specifics — one honest domestic artifact
beats any slogan. Pick one.

## Best for

Food makers, craft sellers, bed-and-breakfasts.
