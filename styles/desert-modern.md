# Desert Modern

> Palm Springs at 10am: shade, breeze-block shadows, and a pool doing all
> the talking.

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|

| `--surface` | `#faf5e8` | Interior white — cards, listings |
| `--ink` | `#33291f` | Walnut — text |

## Type

- **Display:** DM Serif Display 400 — elegant and sun-warmed; headlines read
  like a magazine masthead, not a shout.
- **Body:** DM Sans 400 — clean, quiet descriptions. Keep sentences short.
- **Mono:** Space Mono — square footage, coordinates, lot numbers, dates
  (`2,400 sq ft — 33.83° N`).

Google Fonts: `DM+Serif+Display:ital@0;1&family=DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,400;9..40,500&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Wide margins; the layout breathes like an open floor plan. Let sections
  have real whitespace.
- Breeze-block (CMU) screen patterns as decorative texture — geometric shadow
  grids, used sparingly.
- Radius: `0` on cards, `999px` on the sun logo mark. Long horizontal rule
  lines segment sections.
- Photography does the heavy lifting: one huge architectural image per page.

## Motion

Slow crossfades, long gentle parallax on hero photography. Shadow patterns
drift a few degrees on scroll like the sun is moving. Nothing should move
faster than someone strolling to the pool.

## Do

- One oversized serif headline over a full-bleed architectural photo
- Breeze-block shadow patterns as section dividers
- Pool blue reserved for prices, addresses, and "schedule a tour"
- Property listings with mono spec lines under each photo

## Don't

- No gradients of any kind — the desert is flat light
- No clutter: if a section has more than three elements, remove one
- No rounded cards; modernism has corners
- No stock "luxury" copy — say "3 bed, 2 bath, built 1962" like you mean it

## The one weird thing

One mid-century detail carried everywhere: a starburst divider, a boomerang
motif, or every price set in the mono font like a typed appraisal. Pick one.

## Best for

Real estate, hotels, interior designers, vacation rentals, architecture
studios.

## Lineage

The compositional calm of mid-century architectural photography; the way a
good house tour lets the rooms speak and the agent stays quiet.
