# Dark Luxe

> Near-black, champagne accents, whisper-thin serif. Expensive by restraint.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0e0d0b` | Warm near-black |
| `--surface` | `#161411` | Cards, slightly lifted |
| `--ink` | `#ece5d8` | Warm off-white text |
| `--muted` | `#8a8177` | Secondary text |
| `--accent` | `#c9a96a` | Champagne — hairlines, numerals, one CTA |
| `--line` | `#ece5d822` | Faint warm rules |

## Type

- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond 300/400, large, generous leading, italic accents
- **Body:** Outfit 300/400, `1.7` line-height — air is the luxury
- **Mono:** Space Mono, tiny, tracked-out labels

Google Fonts: `Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,300;0,400;1,300;1,400`
`Outfit:wght@300;400;500` `Space+Mono:wght@400`

## Spacing & shape

- Density is low: `128–200px` section padding. Let it breathe.
- Radius: `0–4px`. Sharp or barely softened.
- Thin champagne hairlines (`1px`) frame key moments — dividers, card edges.

## Motion

Slow and confident: 900ms ease-out reveals, gentle parallax on hero imagery
(max 8% translate), soft glow pulses. Nothing snaps.

## Do

- Full-bleed dark imagery with warm color grade
- Numerals and labels in champagne; body text stays off-white
- One dramatic full-viewport moment (hero or CTA), then restraint
- Letter-spaced uppercase eyebrows in muted tone

## Don't

- No pure white (`#fff`) anywhere — warmth is the whole point
- No neon, no saturated color, no gradients except subtle warm vignettes
- No dense dashboards; if you need density, pick another DNA

## The one weird thing

A single oversized italic word, a champagne rule that draws itself on scroll,
or a full-bleed image with one line of type. Whisper, don't shout.

## Best for

Hospitality, fashion, real estate, premium services, evening brands.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-fullbleed`, `cta`, `testimonials`, `footer`
