# Sauna Cedar

> Cedar heat and steam: a Finnish löyly rendered in dark wood and glowing ember.
> The page should feel like the room goes quiet when the water hits the stones.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#2b1a0e` | Dark cedar shadow background |
| `--surface` | `#3e2614` | Bench-wood panels, inset cards |
| `--ink` | `#f3e6d0` | Steam-white text |
| `--muted` | `#a98d68` | Cedar-dust captions and labels |
| `--accent` | `#e07b39` | Heat glow — one ember detail per section, never fields of it |
| `--line` | `#e07b3933` | Warm hairline rules (alpha allowed on lines) |

## Type

- **Display:** Fraunces (soft, large, sentence case — warmth, never a shout)
- **Body:** Karla (1–1.125rem, unhurried leading; plainspoken like a sauna regular)
- **Mono:** IBM Plex Mono (temperatures, timers, session lengths — "86°C · 12 MIN")

Google Fonts: `Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144;300;9..144;600` `Karla:wght@400;600`
`IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500`

## Spacing & shape

- Generous, even slow: `py-32 md:py-48`. Heat does not rush.
- Radius: `10px` — softened like worn bench wood, never sharp, never pill.
- One idea per viewport; sections breathe apart with wide steam-white gaps of nothing.
- Symmetry is calm and centered; asymmetry would raise the heart rate.

## Motion

The page breathes. Accent glows fade in and out over 4 seconds (opacity only,
never scale). Steam wisps rise on the hero (opacity + translateY, slow).
Everything else enters with a long 600ms fade. Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion`: the glow holds still, the steam never rises.

## Do

- Put temperatures in mono and let them work: "86°C — the stones are ready."
- Teach the ritual as numbered steps: "1. Warm up · 2. Löyly · 3. Cool down · 4. Rest."
- Reserve heat-glow orange for ember moments: one button, one number, one rule per section.
- Write like the room sounds: "Bucket's full. Ladle at will. Quiet hours 7–9am."

## Don't

- No bright white backgrounds, no icy blues — this is heat, not a spa brochure.
- No urgency, no countdown timers, no all-caps shouting. The sauna has no deadline.
- No gradients washing across the page; glow is a detail, not a sky.
- No lorem — warmth with fake copy reads as a template with the heat off.

## The one weird thing

Make the hero a löyly readout: one huge mono temperature ("86°C") in
steam-white with a single ember-orange underline, and beneath it the line
"The stones are ready. So are you." Everything else on the page stays quiet
so the number feels like the room itself.

## Lineage

Finnish sauna culture and cedar-lined löyly rooms; the slow ritual sequence of
heat, steam, cold, rest; ember light as the only ornament in a dark wooden
room. Not cottage-warm: that is blankets-and-tea coziness in the living room;
this is heat on skin — the body, not the sofa. Not scandi-calm: that is pale
birch and white air; this is dark cedar, steam, and ember glow. Principles
only — 100% original implementation.
