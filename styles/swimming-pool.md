# Swimming Pool

> Chlorinated summer: pale water, hot concrete, the lifeguard's red stand.
> Everything smells like sunscreen and the deep end is always colder than you remember.

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|

| `--surface` | `#d8ebe8` | Deeper water — schedule bands |
| `--ink` | `#123a3f` | Deep-end ink |
| `--muted` | `#5c7a7d` | Chlorine haze, secondary text |

## Type

- **Display:** Archivo Black (uppercase pool-deck signage — "NO DIVING", 3–6rem, tight)
- **Body:** Karla (1rem, plainspoken, like the rules board)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (lane times and hours — "Lane 4 · 25 m", "Adult swim 12:00")

Google Fonts: `Archivo+Black` `Karla:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Lane lines: thin horizontal rules separate content like lane dividers.
- Big, airy margins — the deck is wide, let the page breathe.
- Schedules sit in the deeper aqua surface tone, like the shaded end of the pool.
- Radius: `2px`. Pool tile, not pebbles.

## Motion

Lane dividers drift almost imperceptibly on scroll (transform only, one slow loop).
Headlines arrive with a single clean ripple — one ease-out, then still.
With reduced motion: still water.

## Do

- Write the rules board like a human: "Showers before you swim. We mean it."
- Set real schedules as honest mono tables — "Deep end opens Saturday."
- Spend the lifeguard red once per viewport: the whistle or the CTA, never both.
- Microcopy from the deck: "Lap lanes 2 and 3 after 5. Lane 1 is for walking."

## Don't

- No purple or blue gradients — pool water is flat pale aqua, never a gradient sky.
- No beach kitsch: no flip-flop icons, no wave borders, nothing inflatable.
- No emoji icons; no lorem — the pool keeps real hours.
- No dark moody overlays. This is 2 p.m. in July; let it be bright.

## The one weird thing

A water-temperature gauge pinned in the header — "Water: 78°F" — set
differently per page, in mono. When it reads above 84°F, the copy gets a
little drowsy: longer lines, warmer words, like the shallow end at 3 p.m.
