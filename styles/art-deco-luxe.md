# Art Deco Luxe

> 1920s geometric glamour: black lacquer, gold leaf, sunburst symmetry.
> A jazz-age ballroom rendered as a website.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0d0b08` | Near-black lacquer background |
| `--surface` | `#171310` | Panels, cards, inset frames |
| `--ink` | `#f2e7c9` | Warm ivory text |
| `--muted` | `#8a7f63` | Muted champagne, captions and labels |
| `--accent` | `#c9a227` | Antique gold — dividers, rules, keylines, one sunburst |
| `--line` | `#c9a2274d` | Hairline gold rules (alpha allowed on lines) |

## Type

- **Display:** Cinzel (all caps, `0.22em` letter-spacing, never sentence case)
- **Body:** Jost (1–1.1rem, generous leading; sentence case, unhurried)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (prices, dates, coordinates — uppercase, `0.1em` tracking)

Google Fonts: `Cinzel:wght@400;700` `Jost:wght@300;400;500`
`Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Symmetry is mandatory: every section mirrors on a vertical center axis.
- Thick-thin double rules frame sections; hairline gold rules separate.
- Radius: `0px`. Deco is carved, not rounded.
- Rhythm: generous whitespace, then a dense gold-framed inset block. Alternation, never monotony.

## Motion

Motion should feel like a door swinging open, not a phone scrolling. Panels
fade and rise on a slow ease (800–1200ms). Gold rules draw themselves across
the screen. Scrolling between sections can feel like a reveal, not a slide.
Nothing bounces, nothing spins. Luxury is slow.

## Do

- Frame the hero like an invitation card: gold border, centered sunburst or fan motif, one line of type.
- Use hairline gold dividers as the primary structural element between sections.
- Let numbers (prices, dates, addresses) do the working in Space Mono — deco loves an engraved figure.
- Reserve gold for emphasis: one sunburst, one rule-set, one accent word per section. Restraint makes it shine.

## Don't

- No rounded corners, ever — not on buttons, not on images.
- No purple, no neon, no gradients: gold sits on black as flat foil or fine hairline.
- No left-aligned everything; centered ceremonial layout is the default.
- No more than two typefaces on screen at once; Cinzel and Jost carry the page.

## The one weird thing

Add one engraved detail per page: a tiny brass plaque credit line in the
footer ("Est. 2026 · Set in Cinzel"), a coordinate pair under the hero
headline, or a numbered "No. 04" on each section. Deco was obsessed with the
engraved mark — serial numbers, maker stamps, plaques. Pick one.

## Best for

Boutique hotels, jewelers, event spaces, cocktail bars.

## Lineage

The symmetry and sunburst discipline of 1920s poster and typography work; the
restraint of a single gold foil on black board; the ceremonial centered layout
of engraved invitations. Principles only — 100% original implementation.
