# Bike Repair

> Workbench hours: grease under the nails, a truing stand that never lies,
> and the quiet pride of a drivetrain that finally shifts clean.

## Type

- **Display:** Chakra Petch (industrial, mechanical — "TUNE-UP $65", 2–5rem, uppercase)
- **Body:** Public Sans (1rem, work-order plain talk)
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono (specs and tickets — "Torque: 5 N·m · WO-118")

Google Fonts: `Chakra+Petch:wght@400;600;700` `Public+Sans:wght@400;500;700` `JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500`

## Spacing & shape

- Work-order density: honest rows of services with prices in mono.
- Torque charts and gear tables set tight, like the chart taped to the wall.
- Sections separated by workbench-edge rules; nothing floats.
- Radius: `2px`. Cut metal, not molded plastic.

## Motion

Hover states only — a bolt diagram highlights (opacity) when you point at
the service. Nothing scrolls, nothing parallaxed.
With reduced motion: identical, since it barely moves anyway.

## Do

- Talk like the work order: "Bring it in grinding, take it out silent."
- Price every service in mono — the wall chart is public and proud.
- Spend the safety amber once per viewport: the torque marks or the CTA.
- Microcopy from the bench: "Drop it off by five, ride it by Friday."

## Don't

- No purple or blue gradients — the shop is flat wall and steel, never a gradient.
- No cycling kitsch: no winged-wheel clip art, no Tour de France posters.
- No emoji icons; no lorem — the work orders have real numbers.
- No glossy product shots with dark overlays. Show the bench, the tools, the grease.

## The one weird thing

A chainring bolt diagram — five bolts, drawn in line art — where each bolt
is a service link. Hover a bolt and the service highlights. One diagram,
the whole menu, no list.

## Best for

Bike shops, repair services, cycling clubs, makers.
