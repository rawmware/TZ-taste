# Bauhaus Primary

> Functional geometry: red, yellow, blue on off-white.
> A workshop poster that refuses to decorate.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f4f1ea` | Off-white paper stock |
| `--surface` | `#e9e4d6` | Inset panels, table fills |
| `--ink` | `#1a1a1a` | Near-black ink for all type |
| `--muted` | `#6e6a60` | Warm gray, labels and captions |
| `--accent` | `#d8342c` | Primary red — the working color, CTAs, key rules |
| `--accent-2` | `#1f4fa3` | Primary blue — secondary highlights, frames |
| `--line` | `#1a1a1a` | Heavy black rules and grid lines |

## Type

- **Display:** Archivo Black (huge, tight, sentence-case or uppercase — headlines are the graphic)
- **Body:** Archivo (400/500, 1.05rem; plain-spoken, no flourish)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (form labels, measurements, spec data)

Google Fonts: `Archivo+Black` `Archivo:wght@400;500;700`
`Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- The grid is visible: heavy black rules (2–4px) divide the page into honest rectangles.
- Circles, triangles, squares are first-class citizens — geometry replaces illustration.
- Radius: `0px` everywhere. Shape comes from composition, not rounding.
- Density is high but never messy: every element sits on the grid, every color has a job.

## Motion

Motion is mechanical and honest: elements snap into place, panels wipe in,
color blocks slide like stage flats. Eases are linear or sharp — no float, no
bounce. A hover state changes color instantly and boldly. The page should feel
assembled, not animated.

## Do

- Compose with the three primaries as tools: red for action, blue for structure, yellow for the one graphic that must be seen.
- Let massive display type do the visual work — oversized headlines at full column width.
- Use thick black rules and geometric frames to organize content into cells.
- State things plainly in short, imperative copy ("Sit. Build. Learn.") — the workshop voice.

## Don't

- No curves except literal circles; no organic or decorative shapes.
- No gradients, shadows, or depth — flat planes only.
- No more than the three primaries plus black plus paper on any one screen.
- No pastel, no muted versions of the primaries — colors at full strength or not at all.

## The one weird thing

Include one workshop annotation per page: a small dimension line with a
measurement ("450 × 450", "grid: 12"), a color-chip label ("ROT 01"), or an
assembly-style number. The Bauhaus was a school of making — an honesty stamp
that says "this was built, not decorated." Pick one.

## Lineage

The workshop pragmatism of the Bauhaus masters' teaching posters; the
constructivist use of pure primary color as information; the honesty of a grid
you can see. Principles only — 100% original implementation.
