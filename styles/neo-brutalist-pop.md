# Neo-Brutalist Pop

> Thick borders, hard shadows, sticker colors. Playful but engineered.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#fff6e9` | Warm cream |
| `--surface` | `#ffffff` | Cards |
| `--ink` | `#1a1a1a` | Outlines, text |
| `--muted` | `#6b6259` | Secondary |
| `--accent` | `#ff5da2` | Hot pink — primary pop |
| `--accent-2` | `#4d7cff` | Electric blue — secondary pop |
| `--accent-3` | `#ffc93c` | Sticker yellow — highlights |
| `--line` | `#1a1a1a` | 2–3px outlines on everything |

## Type

- **Display:** Archivo Black, tight, often with highlight behind words
- **Body:** Space Grotesk 400/500
- **Mono:** Space Mono for labels and prices

Google Fonts: `Archivo+Black` `Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Hard shadows: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0 var(--ink)`. Hover: `6px 6px 0`.
- Radius: `12–16px` cards, `999px` buttons. Borders `2px` solid ink minimum.
- Stickers: rotated badges with thick borders overlapping card corners.

## Motion

Snappy and bouncy: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` pops, wiggle on hover,
confetti-burst on success. Joyful, never janky.

## Do

- Sticker badges ("NEW", "FREE", "HOT") rotated `-6deg`
- Highlight-marker effect behind key headline words
- Chunky toggle switches and radio cards
- Doodle underlines (SVG squiggles) on links

## Don't

- No muted corporate palettes — commit to the color
- No thin 1px borders; if it's not chunky it's not this DNA
- No more than 3 pop colors per page or it becomes noise

## The one weird thing

A mascot moment, a sticker that follows the cursor, or one section with a
wild repeating pattern background. Playful needs one unhinged beat.

## Best for

Consumer startups, edtech, creator tools, food brands, Gen-Z products.

## Pairs with patterns

`pricing`, `cards`, `cta`, `marquee`
