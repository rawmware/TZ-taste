# Fishing Tackle

> Retail wall craft: pegboard, price guns, and a thousand small decisions behind glass.
> The lure wall is organized by someone who takes this very seriously.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f2efe6` | Pegboard card |
| `--surface` | `#e4ddc9` | Darker card — shelf bands |
| `--ink` | `#23301f` | Tackle-box ink |
| `--muted` | `#6d7360` | River-mud gray, secondary text |
| `--accent` | `#d9480f` | Blaze orange — price tags, one per page |
| `--line` | `#23301f1f` | Peg-hole hairlines (alpha allowed on lines) |

## Type

- **Display:** Oswald (pegboard headers, condensed uppercase — "LIVE BAIT", 2–4rem)
- **Body:** Work Sans (1rem, shelf-talk plain talk)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (prices and SKUs — "$6.49 · SK-2210")

Google Fonts: `Oswald:wght@400;500;700` `Work+Sans:wght@400;500;700` `Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Pegboard grid: dense, honest rows of product cards with price tags.
- Price tickets hang off card corners like they were just gunned on.
- Sections banded in the darker card tone, like shelf levels.
- Radius: `3px`. Blister-pack corners.

## Motion

Price tickets give a small tilt on hover (transform only) — the wobble of a
tag on a wire hook. Nothing else moves.
With reduced motion: tags hang straight and still.

## Do

- Write shelf talk like a human: "The trout are biting on the green ones. Ask Ray."
- Price everything in mono — tackle shops never hide the price.
- Spend the blaze orange once per viewport: the price tags or the CTA.
- Microcopy from the counter: "Minnows are in. They go fast on Saturdays."

## Don't

- No purple or blue gradients — the shop wall is flat card, never a gradient.
- No fishing kitsch: no leaping-bass clip art, no "gone fishin'" signs.
- No emoji icons; no lorem — the lure wall has real SKUs.
- No fake outdoorsy hero photo with a dark overlay. This is a shop, not a lake.

## The one weird thing

A rotating "tackle tip of the day" pegboard ticket — "TIP #12: Tie the Palomar,
cut the tag end close" — gunned onto the page in mono, different every day,
like the owner writes a fresh one each morning.
