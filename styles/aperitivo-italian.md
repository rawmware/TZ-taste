# Aperitivo Italian

> Golden hour at a Milan bar, every night. Campari red, bitter orange, and
> typography you can taste.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f7efdc` | Aged poster paper — warm cream |
| `--surface` | `#fdf7ea` | Clean menu-card cream |
| `--ink` | `#2b1f14` | Espresso — headlines, rules |
| `--muted` | `#a08872` | Cork — captions, fine print |
| `--accent` | `#c93a2e` | Campari red — banners, prices, CTAs |
| `--accent-2` | `#e8822a` | Bitter orange — sun bursts, highlights |
| `--line` | `#e3d3b8` | Menu dividers, stamp boxes |

## Type

- **Display:** Alfa Slab One 400 — all headlines; set huge, tight, and never
  below 48px on desktop. It carries the whole brand.
- **Body:** DM Sans 400/500 — menu descriptions, short stories, plain talk.
- **Mono:** Space Mono — prices (`18:00 — 19:30`), times, "dal 1958" labels,
  fine print.

Google Fonts: `Alfa+Slab+One&family=DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,400;9..40,500;9..40,700&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700`

## Spacing & shape

- Full-bleed red bands split sections like poster strips; cream fields in
  between.
- Double hairline rules (`1px` + `3px` pair) frame menus and price lists.
- Badges are circles or ovals — "Aperol", "Est. 1958", "Lunedì" — slightly
  rotated, stamp-like.
- Radius: `2px` on cards, `999px` on badge pills. Generous padding; posters
  never feel cramped.

## Motion

Badges wobble once on entrance like a freshly hung sign. Bands slide in with
a letterpress thump — fast settle, no bounce. Hover on a menu row flips the
price into red. Motion should feel like the place is filling up, not like the
page is showing off.

## Do

- Giant slab headlines over cream, one red word pulled out per line
- Alternating bands: red field with cream text / cream field with espresso text
- Menu-style price lists with dotted leaders between item and price
- Rotated oval badges: "Dalle 18:00", "Grande festa", "Senza glutine"

## Don't

- No photography filters — if you use photos, let them be bright and warm
- No pastel versions of red; Campari red is a commitment, not a suggestion
- No more than two fonts of display-size text per section; the slab already
  shouts
- No centered paragraphs longer than two lines — ragged edges feel human

## The one weird thing

Pick one: a huge half-sun or citrus-slice arc that bleeds off a section edge,
or every price written in the local hand with a `Space Mono` timestamp next to
it ("17:45 — happy"). Pick one.

## Best for

Bars, restaurants, food brands, trattorias, wine labels.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `nav-pill`, `marquee`, `cta`, `background-grain`, `cards`

## Lineage

The flat-color confidence of 1960s Milanese bar posters; the economy of a
handwritten daily-specials board where nothing wasted space.
