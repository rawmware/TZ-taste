# Scandi Calm

> Hygge minimalism: warm whites, pale wood tones, quiet functionality.
> Nothing shouts; everything breathes.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#faf7f1` | Warm white, like fresh paint on pine |
| `--surface` | `#f1ece1` | Soft linen panels, cards |
| `--ink` | `#2e2a25` | Warm charcoal text |
| `--muted` | `#9a917f` | Stone gray, captions and hints |
| `--accent` | `#a9805a` | Pale wood tone — CTAs, highlights, one warm touch |
| `--line` | `#e4dccb` | Faint seam lines, barely there |

## Type

- **Display:** DM Serif Display (large, relaxed, sentence case — elegant without effort)
- **Body:** DM Sans (400, 1.05–1.1rem, 1.7 line-height; reads like a quiet conversation)
- **Mono:** Space Mono (rare here — only for product codes or sizes, small)

Google Fonts: `DM+Serif+Display` `DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,400;500`
`Space+Mono:wght@400`

## Spacing & shape

- Generous whitespace is the main design element. When in doubt, add more air.
- Radius: soft, `10–16px` on cards; nothing sharp, nothing bubbly.
- Imagery floats on the background with no heavy frames — product on cream, like a catalog.
- Rhythm: even, measured, one idea per viewport. No crowding, no stacking.

## Motion

Barely there. Fades only, slow and soft (600–900ms). Hover states warm the
color slightly rather than moving. No entrances, no parallax, no playfulness.
The page should feel like it was always there — you just noticed it.

## Do

- Use one warm material tone (pale wood) as the only color story — everything else is white, cream, stone.
- Write copy like a good host: short, warm, practical. "This one fits a small hall." "Machine-washable. Obviously."
- Let imagery be airy and daylight-lit; products photographed on light backgrounds.
- Keep the layout column-narrow and centered; quiet pages don't sprawl.

## Don't

- No black backgrounds, no neon, no loud colors — one warm accent is the entire palette.
- No tight grids of tiny cards; give every item room to breathe.
- No urgency copy ("Only 3 left!"), no exclamation marks, no countdown timers.
- No drop shadows with visible offset — if you shadow at all, it must be a soft ambient blur.

## The one weird thing

Add one cozy detail per page: a tiny "made for slow mornings" caption under
the hero image, a coffee-ring-like warm circle mark, or a handwritten-style
note in the footer. Hygge is about felt comfort — one human domestic touch
that isn't furniture. Pick one.

## Best for

Homeware brands, wellness studios, interior designers.
