# Ma (Japanese Minimal)

> Rice paper, ink black, vast emptiness used on purpose. Ma — the beauty of
> negative space.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f7f4ec` | Rice paper |
| `--surface` | `#efe9da` | Slightly deeper paper |
| `--ink` | `#26221c` | Sumi ink |
| `--muted` | `#8a8478` | Stone gray |
| `--accent` | `#a33327` | Shu-iro red — the hanko stamp, used once or twice |
| `--line` | `#26221c1f` | Whisper rules |

## Type

- **Display:** Shippori Mincho, light weights, enormous — or a single kanji as art
- **Body:** Zen Kaku Gothic New 400, `1.9` line-height — text needs room
- **Mono:** Space Mono for the rare label

Google Fonts: `Shippori+Mincho:wght@400;600;800` `Zen+Kaku+Gothic+New:wght@400;500;700`
`Space+Mono:wght@400`

## Spacing & shape

- The emptiest DNA here: `160–240px` section padding, single-column narrow
  measures, vast margins.
- Radius: `0`. Enso-circle imagery instead of rounded UI.
- Vertical Japanese text accents (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) as decoration.

## Motion

Barely-there: 1200ms fades, ink-brush reveals (clip-path wipes), slow drifting
mist. Stillness is the feature.

## Do

- One red hanko-stamp element per page (logo mark, section seal)
- Full-bleed negative space — don't fill every corner
- Seasonal, natural imagery: stone, water, paper texture
- Numbered chapters like a book, not sections like a template

## Don't

- No gradients, no shadows, no cards-with-borders aesthetic
- No exclamation marks in copy. Understatement only.
- No density — if content is dense, this is the wrong DNA

## The one weird thing

A single vertical line of Japanese text, an enso circle drawn on load, or one
deliberately off-center element in all the symmetry of emptiness.

## Best for

Craft brands, architecture, tea/food, galleries, meditation products.

## Pairs with patterns

`hero-editorial`, `testimonials`, `footer`
