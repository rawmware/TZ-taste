# Prompt: Brand refresh

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Design a brand refresh for [COMPANY NAME], a [WHAT THEY DO] whose current brand feels [CURRENT PROBLEM, e.g. "dated, corporate, forgettable"].

- **Audit snapshot first:** name 3 things the old brand gets wrong and 1 thing worth keeping. The refresh builds on the keeper.
- **Logo rules:** show the mark at three sizes, state minimum size and clearspace, and show 3 real misuses (stretched, recolored, busy background) marked as wrong.
- **Color tokens:** [NUMBER] colors max, each with a name, a hex, and a job (background / text / accent). One accent only — if it's everywhere, it's nowhere.
- **Type pairing:** one display + one body from the DNA, with a live scale (display / h1 / h2 / body / small / micro) set in real headlines from the company's copy.
- **Voice before/after:** rewrite [2 SAMPLES OF THEIR CURRENT COPY] in the new voice. State the voice in 3 adjectives and 1 thing it never does.
- **Do / don't board:** 6 pairs — same content, one execution on-brand, one off. Make the wrong ones visibly tempting.
- **Applications:** the new brand applied to [3 REAL SURFACES, e.g. "homepage hero, invoice, work van"] — not floating logo mockups.
- **Photography / illustration direction:** 3 reference images, each with a one-line art-direction rule — [e.g. "real customers, natural light, no stock smiles"].
- **The rename, if any:** if the name itself changes, state the reason in one sentence and show old/new side by side. Rebrands die on mystery.
- **Rollout order:** where the old brand gets replaced first, ranked by visibility — [e.g. "homepage, invoices, email signature"].
- **Constraints:** real copy, no lorem ipsum; renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Do NOT build: a gradient-mesh everything; jargon mission statements ("synergy-driven excellence" style); three equal brand-value cards; a mood board of stock photos; Inter as the body font. If the refresh could be pasted onto any company, redo it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the refresh's one distinctive choice.
