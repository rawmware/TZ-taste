# Prompt: Testimonials page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build the customer-proof page for [PRODUCT NAME], a [WHAT IT DOES] used by [AUDIENCE].

- **Full-length stories, not snippets:** [3–5 CASES] each with a real name, real role, real company — plus the before/after in their own words. Anonymous "CEO, SaaS company" quotes don't ship.
- **Specificity is the proof:** every story names a concrete number, date, or situation — [e.g. "cut invoice time from 3 days to 40 minutes"]. Vague praise ("great product!") gets cut or rewritten.
- **Filter by reader:** let visitors sort stories by [USE CASE / COMPANY SIZE / INDUSTRY] so each reader finds someone like them in one click.
- **Video with transcripts:** [1–2 VIDEO TESTIMONIALS] embedded with full transcripts below — transcripts are non-negotiable for access and for skimmers.
- **Honest sourcing:** each quote states when it was given and in what context ([e.g. "from a support survey, March 2026"]). No invented quotes, no stock-photo avatars.
- **One conversion job:** the page exists to [PRIMARY GOAL, e.g. "book a demo"]. Repeat a single contextual CTA after the strongest stories — not a different button every section.
- **Freshness:** stories carry dates; re-verify or rotate anything older than [e.g. 18 months]. Stale proof reads as a dead product.
- **Permission stated:** note that every featured customer approved their story — [e.g. "shared with permission"]. Credibility lives in the sourcing.
- **Reading rhythm:** one story per viewport on mobile, quotes set at a 45–75 character measure. Proof needs air around it.
- **Constraints:** [stack, e.g. "React + Tailwind"]; real copy, no lorem ipsum; renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Do NOT build: three identical quote cards with five gold stars; a "wall of love" grid of one-liners; stock avatars; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font; autoplaying video. If every quote could be about any product, rewrite them before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one distinctive choice.
