# Prompt: 404 page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit,
Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md
protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA
from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY
dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build a 404 (page not found) error page for [SITE NAME / BRAND VOICE].

- **Brand voice:** [3 adjectives — e.g. "dry, precise, a little tired"]
- **Most-visited pages:** [3–5 real URLs to offer as the way out]
- **Search:** [does the site have search? If yes, wire a real search box]
- **Report link:** [optional — a way to tell the team the link broke]

**Structure (in this order):**
1. The fact, plainly: this page doesn't exist. Big type, no euphemism.
2. ONE gag or image — the site's personality showing, not a stock
   "confused robot" illustration. One. Not a gallery of jokes.
3. The way out: the short list of real links above, plus a search box if the
   site has search.
4. Optional: one line inviting the user to report the dead link, with a link.
5. The normal site footer — an error page still ends like the site does.

**Rules:**
- The status is obvious in under one second. No one should wonder what
   happened.
- Humor must land in the brand voice, not in a different brand's voice.
   Read the joke out loud: if it could belong to any company, cut it.
- Everything on the page works: links go somewhere, the search searches.
- No fake sitemap, no dumping the entire nav, no "OOPS!" as the whole joke.
- If the site has no search, do not show a search box. Never fake function.
- The HTTP status stays 404 for crawlers — note this in your reply.
- Copy ban: "Oops!", "Something went wrong", "Let's get you back on track".

Do NOT build: a centered hero with badge + headline + two buttons; three equal
feature cards; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font. If the output
resembles a template, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose
and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one
distinctive choice — and then state the gag out loud so I can veto it before
you build around it.
