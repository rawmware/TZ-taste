# Prompt: About page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit,
Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md
protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA
from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY
dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build an about page for [COMPANY NAME].

- **Founded:** [year, and why — the honest version]
- **What they do:** [one sentence]
- **The real story:** [2–3 concrete facts: where it started, who started it,
  what almost killed it. No myths.]
- **Team:** [number of people, and who they are — names and roles, or a
  statement that it's one person]
- **Voice:** [3 adjectives — e.g. "plainspoken, wry, earnest"]

**Structure (in this order):**
1. A single strong opener — a sentence or a number, not a paragraph about
   "our journey".
2. One real photograph or portrait of the founder/team. (If no photo exists,
   design the space for one — do not fake it with a stock "team high-five"
   image.)
3. The story in short sections with dates that mean something.
4. Three numbers that are true: customers, years, locations — with context.
5. A values block with ONE sentence each, written like a human said them.
6. The next step: one contact link or hiring link, matching their actual
   openness.

**Rules:**
- No "Our Story" hero with a blurred stock photo and a white headline.
- No grid of six identical "value cards" with icons.
- The headline states what the company actually believes, e.g.
  "We think most [X] is overbuilt."
- Team section: if there are fewer than 8 people, show names and faces or
  skip the section. Never a stock photo labeled "our team".
- Every claim must be verifiable. "Best-in-class" is not a claim.
- Copy ban: "passionate", "leverage", "cutting-edge", "synergy", "world-class".

Do NOT build: a centered hero with badge + headline + two buttons; three equal
feature cards; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font. If the output
resembles a template, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose
and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one
distinctive choice.
