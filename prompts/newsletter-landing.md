# Prompt: Newsletter landing page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build the subscribe page for [NEWSLETTER NAME], a [FREQUENCY, e.g. "weekly"] newsletter about [TOPIC] for [READER].

- **Read before you subscribe:** [2–3 FULL SAMPLE ISSUES] readable on the page, no signup required. The samples are the pitch — write them like the real thing.
- **Cadence promise:** state exactly how often it arrives, on which day, and roughly how long each issue is. [e.g. "Every Tuesday, ~5 minutes."]
- **Honest audience line:** say who it's for and who should skip it. [e.g. "For junior devs. Senior architects will be bored."]
- **Archive:** past issues listed by date with real titles, searchable or filterable by [TOPIC TAG]. The archive proves it ships.
- **Unsubscribe up front:** state the unsubscribe promise before the form — one click, no guilt copy, [e.g. "your data deleted"]. Trust is the conversion mechanic here.
- **The form:** email field only, button that says what happens next ("Send me Tuesday's issue"). Success state shows what arrives when; error states name the problem plainly.
- **Honest social proof:** reader quotes only with real names and roles — or skip the section. Fabricated praise poisons the pitch.
- **Referral mechanics:** if a referral program exists, state the reward and threshold plainly — [e.g. "3 referrals gets a shoutout in the issue"].
- **Inbox preview:** show the email inside a real inbox-client frame, not a floating browser mockup.
- **Constraints:** [stack, e.g. "React + Tailwind"]; real copy, no lorem ipsum; renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Do NOT build: "Join 10,000+ smart readers" or any unverifiable subscriber count; a fake testimonial carousel; a stock photo of someone reading on a couch; a purple/blue gradient; three equal benefit cards; Inter as the body font. If the page asks for the email before showing the goods, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one distinctive choice.
