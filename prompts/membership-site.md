# Prompt: Membership site page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit,
Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md
protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA
from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY
dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build a membership join page for [COMMUNITY NAME] — [what members get:
e.g. "a private community + monthly workshops for freelance designers"].

- **The promise:** [one sentence — what changes for members in 90 days]
- **Audience:** [who, and what they're currently missing]
- **Feeling:** [3 adjectives — e.g. "belonging, craft, momentum"]
- **Tiers:** [names + prices + the real differences, max 3]
- **Sections:** belonging-led hero → what's inside (the actual artifacts:
  channels, calls, library, events) → member proof (names, results, quotes) →
  event calendar preview → tier picker → what happens after joining (day-one
  email, first call date) → FAQ (cancel, pause, guests) → footer
- **Constraints:** [stack, e.g. "React + Tailwind"]; real copy, no lorem ipsum;
  renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Do NOT build: stock photos of high-fiving teams; "join 10,000+ members" with
no real number; three equal tier cards with checkmark lists that differ by one
line; a centered hero with badge + headline + two buttons; a purple/blue
gradient; Inter as the body font. Memberships sell belonging — show the actual
inside (real channel names, real event titles), not the idea of community.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose
and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one
distinctive choice.
