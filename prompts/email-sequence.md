# Prompt: Email sequence

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Write and design a [WELCOME / NURTURE / RE-ENGAGEMENT] email sequence for [PRODUCT NAME], [LENGTH, e.g. "4 emails over 10 days"].

- **Cadence map first:** list each email with its send trigger ([e.g. "Email 1: immediately after signup"]) and the single job it does. If an email's job overlaps another's, merge them.
- **One idea, one CTA per email.** The reader should be able to act in under 30 seconds. Every email links to [THE ONE ACTION].
- **Subject + preview text** for each email, written like a person: under 50 characters, no clickbait, no emoji, no ALL CAPS. Each must stand alone in an inbox list.
- **Structure:** plain-spoken opener, one concrete proof point or example, one clear ask. Sign each email from [REAL NAME, REAL ROLE] — not "The [Product] Team".
- **First-email payoff:** email 1 delivers something useful immediately — a checklist, a template, an answer — not just "thanks for signing up".
- **Plain-text fallback** for every email. The text version must read as a complete letter, not a stripped afterthought.
- **Replies invited:** every email invites a reply and names who reads it. Replies go to a monitored inbox, never a no-reply address.
- **Timing honesty:** send times stated in the reader's local time; night sends get a stated reason or don't ship.
- **Voice consistency:** the sequence sounds like one person wrote all of it. Keep a banned-phrases list per the slop blocklist for the whole sequence.
- **Deliverability basics:** single-column layout, real text-to-image ratio, bulletproof buttons, unsubscribe link that's easy to find, physical address in the footer, [YOUR SENDER DOMAIN] aligned.
- **Constraints:** table-based layout that degrades cleanly; renders at desktop width and stacks at 390; dark-mode safe colors; real copy, no lorem ipsum.

Do NOT build: identical centered hero blocks with stock imagery; emoji subject lines; slop-blocklist jargon anywhere in the copy (see skill/SKILL.md); a hidden unsubscribe link; fake personalization like "Hey [FIRST NAME]". If the sequence reads like every SaaS drip campaign, rewrite it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the sequence's one distinctive choice.
