# Prompt: Video sales landing page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build a video-first sales page for [PRODUCT NAME], a [WHAT IT DOES] that costs [PRICE].

- **Video first, never trapped:** the [LENGTH, e.g. "12-minute"] video leads the page — hosted on [PLATFORM], click-to-play, never autoplay. Full transcript below it, plus chapter timestamps so skimmers can jump.
- **The offer box:** price, exactly what's included ([LIST THE DELIVERABLES]), and what's not. No "value stack" math inventing $4,997 of bonuses for a $97 product.
- **Guarantee in plain language:** state the refund/return terms as you'd explain them to a friend — [e.g. "30 days, email us, no questions, money back in 5 business days"]. If there's no guarantee, say so honestly instead of faking one.
- **Objection FAQ:** [5–7 REAL OBJECTIONS, e.g. "I don't have time", "will this work for my case?"] answered directly, each with a named person or data point behind the answer.
- **Who it's not for:** a short, honest section that disqualifies bad-fit buyers. It costs a few sales and earns the trust that closes the rest.
- **Scarcity only if real:** [e.g. "cohort capped at 40 seats — 12 left"] with the real mechanism stated. No fake countdown timers, no "price goes up at midnight" theater.
- **Page speed:** poster frame loads instantly, player lazy-loads on click. A sales page that stutters sells nothing.
- **Mobile order:** video → offer box → CTA stack in that order at 390. The offer never needs pinching or horizontal scroll.
- **Access:** captions on the video, transcript below, offer box readable by screen reader in logical order.
- **Constraints:** [stack, e.g. "React + Tailwind"]; real copy, no lorem ipsum; renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Do NOT build: a fake countdown timer; red underlined "WATCH NOW" stock imagery; three equal benefit cards; "Doctors hate this" energy; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font; autoplaying video with sound. If the page pressures more than it informs, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one distinctive choice.
