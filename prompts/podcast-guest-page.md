# Prompt: Podcast guest page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit,
Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md
protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA
from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY
dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build a "be a guest" page for [PODCAST NAME] that convinces the right people
to pitch themselves.

- **Show premise:** [one sentence — what the show is about]
- **Audience:** [who should guest — e.g. "founders with a real failure story"]
- **Reach stats:** [real numbers — downloads/episode, listener titles, distribution]
- **Feeling:** [3 adjectives — e.g. "sharp, curious, unpretentious"]
- **Sections:** hook (why guests say yes) → past guests (names + episode topics,
  not headshot grids) → topics we book / topics we don't → what guests get
  (clips, transcript, distribution) → application form (name, topic pitch,
  links, availability) → FAQ (format, length, prep) → footer
- **Constraints:** [stack, e.g. "React + Tailwind"]; real copy, no lorem ipsum;
  renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Do NOT build: inflated stats ("millions of listeners" without proof); a wall of
identical guest cards; a centered hero with badge + headline + two buttons; a
purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font. The "topics we don't book" list
is mandatory — specificity attracts better pitches than openness.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose
and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one
distinctive choice.
