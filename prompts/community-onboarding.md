# Prompt: Community onboarding

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Design the new-member onboarding flow for [COMMUNITY NAME], a [TYPE OF COMMUNITY] for [MEMBERS] hosted on [PLATFORM].

- **Purpose first:** one screen states what this community is for and what it's not for — [e.g. "for sharing works-in-progress; not for job postings"]. Mismatched members are the #1 community killer.
- **Rules in plain language:** [YOUR RULES] rewritten so a tired person at 11pm understands them — 5–7 max, each with one concrete example of the behavior. Link the full code of conduct; don't paste it.
- **Channel / space tour:** walk through [THE SPACES] with one line each on what belongs where. New members post in the wrong place when nobody shows them the map.
- **First-post prompt:** give them a low-stakes, specific first action — [e.g. "introduce yourself with your current project and one thing you're stuck on"]. Blank "say hi" channels die.
- **Moderation norms:** state who moderates, how to report, and what happens on a violation — including the appeal path. Safety you can't see isn't safety.
- **Roles and access:** explain [ROLES / TIERS] honestly — what each unlocks and how to earn it. No fake member counts, no "exclusive" theater around empty rooms.
- **Time-box it:** the whole flow takes under [e.g. 5 minutes] — state that up front. A stated duration is a conversion feature.
- **A named human:** new members get one real person to ask early questions — [e.g. a "welcome crew" role] — not a bot and not a void.
- **Leaving is documented:** state how to leave and what happens to their posts. Communities that trap people rot from the inside.
- **Constraints:** [stack, e.g. "React + Tailwind"]; real copy, no lorem ipsum; renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Do NOT build: a wall of rules text in 10px gray; a "welcome!" hero with stock community photos; three equal value-prop cards; fake "12,408 members online" counters; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font. If a new member couldn't act after reading this, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the flow's one distinctive choice.
