# Prompt: Waitlist page

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit,
Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md
protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA
from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY
dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build a pre-launch waitlist page for [PRODUCT NAME].

- **What it is:** [one sentence — what launches, and when]
- **Launch timing:** [date or window — "Q1" vagueness is allowed if honest]
- **The hook:** [why join now — early access, founding pricing, the actual reason]
- **Referral mechanic:** [how sharing moves you up, and what the reward is]
- **Audience:** [who wants in first, and where they hang out]
- **Feeling:** [3 adjectives — e.g. "electric, exclusive, impatient"]
- **Sections:** teaser → email capture → position/share screen → FAQ →
  footer
- **Constraints:** [stack, e.g. "React + Tailwind"]; real copy, no lorem ipsum;
  renders clean at 1440/768/390; respects prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG AA contrast.

Waitlist rules:

- The email capture asks for email and nothing else. Name, company, and
  "how did you hear about us" are launch-day questions, not signup questions.
- After signup, the user gets their real position number and a personal
  referral link. Position counts are honest — no fake "you're #47!" when
  the list is 12 people deep.
- Referral mechanics are stated as plain rules: "Each friend who joins moves
  you up 5 spots. Top 100 get founding pricing." No mystery tiers, no fine
  print that voids the reward.
- The FAQ answers the questions people actually ask: when does it launch,
  what does it cost, what do I get for waiting, can I leave the list.
- Social proof is real or absent: a live counter ("2,314 waiting"), real
  quotes from beta users with names, or nothing. Never invented testimonials.
- Share buttons use the referral link with pre-written text the user can
  edit. The copy they share should sound like a person, not a growth team.

Do NOT build: a centered hero with badge + headline + two buttons; three equal
"how it works" cards; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font; a fake
countdown timer to a launch date you invented. If the output resembles a
template, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose
and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one
distinctive choice.
