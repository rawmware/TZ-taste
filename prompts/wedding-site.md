# Prompt: Wedding site

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit,
Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md
protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA
from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY
dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build a wedding website for [PARTNER ONE] and [PARTNER TWO].

- **Date & venue:** [exact date, time, venue name, city]
- **How they met:** [the true story in 3–4 sentences]
- **Key people:** [wedding party names and roles — or skip this section]
- **RSVP deadline:** [exact date]
- **Logistics:** [dress code, parking, plus-ones, kids, gifts/registry]

**Structure (in this order):**
1. Names, date, venue, city — the four facts, up top, no scrolling required
   to find them.
2. One good photo of the couple. Real, recent, unposed beats formal.
3. Their story: the true version, short. Funny beats sentimental if that's
   who they are.
4. The details: schedule of the day with times, dress code in plain words
   ("jackets, no ties" not "cocktail attire"), parking/transit, plus-one
   policy, kids policy.
5. Wedding party only if they want it: names and one line each. No
   identical bio cards.
6. Registry: links to the real registries. If there isn't one, say what
   they'd actually like ("your presence, truly" — and mean it).
7. RSVP form: name, attending yes/no, meal choice (the real options),
   dietary notes, song request (optional). Deadline stated on the form.
8. FAQ: the questions guests actually ask — can I bring kids, where do I
   stay, is it outside.
9. Footer: one line, maybe the date again.

**Rules:**
- The four facts (names, date, venue, city) are visible without scrolling
  on every viewport. Guests come for logistics, not poetry.
- RSVP deadline is stated twice: on the form and in the details.
- Meal choices are the real ones with real names. "Chicken / fish /
  vegetarian" is fine if that's the menu.
- One photo section, not a 40-image gallery. This is a website, not a
  slideshow.
- No countdown timer unless the couple asks for one — and they should know
  it reads as stress, not romance.
- No "our love story" framing unless they actually talk like that. Write
  like they talk.
- Copy ban: "happily ever after", "special day", "love birds", "Mr. and
  Mrs.", "tying the knot" (as the headline).

Do NOT build: a centered hero with badge + headline + two buttons; three equal
feature cards; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font. If the output
resembles a template, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose
and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one
distinctive choice.
