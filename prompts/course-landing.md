# Prompt: Course landing

Copy everything below the line into your AI builder (v0, Lovable, Replit,
Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT). Fill in the [BRACKETS] first.

---

Use https://github.com/rawmware/TZ-taste as a reference. Follow its AGENT.md
protocol: state your design read in one sentence, pick exactly ONE style DNA
from styles/index.json, and obey skill/SKILL.md (set VARIANCE/MOTION/DENSITY
dials, zero slop-blocklist violations).

Build a sales page for an online course: [COURSE NAME].

- **Who it's for:** [level and background, e.g. "developers who've shipped
  one production API"]
- **Who it's NOT for:** [say it — e.g. "not for total beginners"]
- **Outcome:** [one concrete skill, e.g. "deploy a multi-region Postgres
  failover yourself"]
- **Format:** [video hours, projects, live sessions, community — exact]
- **Price:** [exact number and what's included; refund policy in plain words]

**Structure (in this order):**
1. Headline stating the outcome, not the topic. "Deploy multi-region
   Postgres" beats "Learn advanced databases".
2. One enrollment CTA with the price next to it. No "from $X", no hidden
   asterisk.
3. The full curriculum: every module with its lessons listed. Nothing
   blurred, nothing "and much more".
4. Who it's for and who it's NOT for — a two-column honest filter that saves
   the wrong people their money.
5. The instructor: real name, real background, one credential that matters.
6. Format details: hours, projects, how long access lasts, refund terms.
7. Testimonials only if they exist with real names — quote the specific
   result, not "life-changing". None is fine; fake ones are not.
8. FAQ that answers the real objections: time commitment, refunds, level.
9. Final CTA repeating the price. Footer.

**Rules:**
- The curriculum is fully visible. A course that hides its syllabus is a red
  flag — design it like you have nothing to hide.
- Price appears twice, exact, with the refund policy within sight of it.
- No fake social proof: no invented review counts, no stock-photo students,
  no "join 10,000+ learners" without a source.
- No "valued at $997, today $99" anchoring games.
- Guarantees are literal: state exactly what the guarantee pays and how to
  claim it.
- Copy ban: "unlock your potential", "master", "skyrocket", "secrets",
  "guru".

Do NOT build: a centered hero with badge + headline + two buttons; three equal
feature cards; a purple/blue gradient; Inter as the body font. If the output
resembles a template, redesign it before showing me.

Before writing code, tell me: your one-sentence design read, the DNA you chose
and why the runner-up loses, your dial settings, and the page's one
distinctive choice.
